Research Agent Lab

This interactive prototype performs local, deterministic comparison of two illustrative system architectures. It demonstrates the evidence schema, comparison logic and rule-based issue detection. It does not fetch live sources, operate autonomous AI agents, test suppliers or certify legal compliance. Everything shown is simulated educational test data, not findings about named products.

Sample system data and comparison rules

Each system has a stable ID, explicit component list, dependency edges, claimed controls, demonstration-only source record and authorisation level. Jaccard similarity is intersection over union for component sets, so 100% component overlap does not establish functional or ethical equivalence. Rules flag lack of approval, unknown logging and absence of versioned citations as review questions, not verified failures.

Engineering gates before real agents
Document parser fidelity; source permission and robots/site restrictions; scoped tool tokens; sandboxed read-only ingestion; privacy and retention; prompt-injection resilience; citation verification; withheld verdicts when evidence is inadequate; benchmark against human-labelled cases; approval before externally visible publication; monitoring, rollback and incident response.
Agent security: OWASP · Evidence provenance: W3C · Risk management: NIST · Systems Atlas
