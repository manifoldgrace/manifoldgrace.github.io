How to use this directory: Find an unresolved question; read the original paper or issue; inspect methods, limitations, reviews and code; compare alternative approaches; then document the gap and a safe, reproducible next experiment. A preprint is not automatically peer-reviewed, an open issue is not a confirmed defect, and a leaderboard is not proof of real-world reliability.

Research discovery and attribution practice

For each promising finding, capture the original authors, publication date, source URL, research problem, existing approaches, evidence, unresolved limitations, alternative designs and the next falsifiable question. Link back to original sources and avoid claiming independent validation where none exists.

These are curated destinations, not a real-time feed, an endorsement or an exhaustive map of all frontier research. Current research may also be unpublished, private or absent from indexed sources. Link availability, project activity and editorial status can change.
