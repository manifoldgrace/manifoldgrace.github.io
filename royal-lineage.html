How to read this diagram: A solid vertical line means the relationship is identified by the current family record. It is not a claim that every supporting certificate has already been collected or that an office has been conferred. A dashed research card means the exact genealogy still needs primary-source investigation. Historic chieftaincy office succession is deliberately not drawn as if each officeholder were the next person's parent.

This page is a research-led visual index. Living relatives' names are deliberately omitted from the diagram; the main heritage archive contains additional biographical and family-history details.
