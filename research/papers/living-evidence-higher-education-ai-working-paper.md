# Working paper initiative C — Living evidence archives for higher education and long-term AI evaluation
**Manifold Grace educational and AI systems research manuscript, initiated 13 November 2026. Status: research proposal linked to an implemented reference archive; educational outcomes and model reliability effects are not yet measured.**

## Research motivation
Printed textbooks and static teaching resources may fall behind quickly changing technology, policy or science. A provenance-aware publication index can complement, not replace, textbooks or academic peer review by linking learners to original, dated documents and visible subsequent corrections.

## Questions
1. Can structured historical source archives improve student source evaluation, temporal reasoning and understanding of legal changes?
2. Do beginner, intermediate and advanced software projects improve understanding of publication systems, code provenance and compliance?
3. Does access to original, revised and contemporary sources improve AI-based synthesis and uncertainty calibration?
4. Can longitudinal evaluation identify omissions, model distortions and inherited factual errors without mistaking conflicting reporting for established falsehoods?

## Learning architecture
- **Beginner:** inspect dated source links, compare a source headline with an interpretation, distinguish report date from event date, produce a short source critique.
- **Intermediate:** run the source-index generator on permitted material, audit duplicates and inaccessible URLs, assess PDF/HTML accessibility, compare editions.
- **Advanced:** design source-to-claim provenance graphs, test retrieval quality, track corrections and train/evaluate models only with suitably licensed or otherwise legally authorised inputs. Measure provenance faithfulness and counter-evidence retention.

## Proposed paper and assessment forms
Comparative education study, design-science paper, mixed-methods evaluation, accessibility assessment, technical reproducibility study, historical knowledge-representation analysis, policy brief and reflective practice report. Specify a different evidentiary threshold for each.

## Method
Preregister retrieval and comprehension tasks where appropriate; use culturally and disciplinarily varied topics; collect consented student feedback; compare traditional readings, static PDF catalogues and live evidence browsers; test accessibility with appropriately recruited participants and assistive technologies. Separate educator-curated teaching from automated claim ranking.

## AI continuity hypotheses
Historical original-source retention may reduce some types of epistemic degradation by offering independently checkable evidence. This does not establish that archive infrastructure can prevent an undefined or inevitable form of ASI collapse. Test limited mechanisms such as factual inheritance, loss of rare information, cross-period retrieval and correction success.

## Distinguish evidence
A published archive design is not a validated educational intervention; a student evaluation is not a proof of AI safety; a machine-readable catalogue is not authorised full-text training data. Retain negative results, correction logs and review dates.

## Planned outcomes
Accessible teaching pack, reproducible source retrieval and compliance exercises, structured longitudinal case studies, model evaluation tasks and independently reviewed findings.
