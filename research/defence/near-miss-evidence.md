# Near misses and selection bias

**Drafted 16 November 2026 · Working research draft**

## Research question

How sensitive are conclusions about escalation to missing encounters that were resolved before a threat, display or use of force was recorded?

## Starting evidence and candidate gap

The Militarized Interstate Disputes dataset applies an explicit threshold. Check which comparable publicly documented encounters fall outside it.

[Starting source](https://correlatesofwar.org/data-sets/mids/)

## Proposed study

Build a small comparison sample and report how alternative inclusion rules affect conclusions. Record coverage limitations for each source.

## Resume here

Search for two recent studies addressing this exact question. Log what they resolve, the population and period covered, and what remains testable. Revise the question if existing evidence already answers it. Record the source search date and selection criteria.
