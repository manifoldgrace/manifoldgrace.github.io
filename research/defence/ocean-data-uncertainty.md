# Ocean observation and uncertainty

**Drafted 16 November 2026 · Published work in progress**

Published work in progress. Research is underway. The questions, evidence and paper structure may change as the work develops.

## Research question

How often does an ocean model communicate enough uncertainty for a reviewer to distinguish poor observation coverage from evidence that an event did not occur?

## Starting evidence and candidate gap

The 2026 Global Ocean Observing System report identifies remaining monitoring gaps. This study would examine how uncertainty appears in downstream reviews.

[Starting source](https://www.ioc.unesco.org/en/articles/global-ocean-observing-system-status-report-2026-released)

## Proposed study

Use public environmental observations and fictional incidents. Compare conclusions before and after showing coverage and uncertainty explicitly.
