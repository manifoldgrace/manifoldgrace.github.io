# Cumulative marine impacts

**Drafted 16 November 2026 · Published work in progress**

Published work in progress. Research is underway. The questions, evidence and paper structure may change as the work develops.

## Research question

How do review bodies document cumulative impacts when activities have different disclosure duties or cross jurisdictional boundaries?

## Starting evidence and candidate gap

Use the BBNJ environmental assessment framework as a baseline. Check scope and exceptions before drawing comparisons.

[Starting source](https://www.un.org/bbnjagreement/sites/default/files/2024-08/BBNJAgreementFactsheet4EIAs.pdf)

## Proposed study

Compare public assessments and code which cumulative effects were considered, who supplied evidence and how information gaps were handled.

## Resume here

Search for two recent studies addressing this exact question. Log what they resolve, the population and period covered, and what remains testable. Revise the question if existing evidence already answers it. Record the source search date and selection criteria.
