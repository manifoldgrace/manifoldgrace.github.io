# AI summaries and inquiry evidence

**Drafted 16 November 2026 · Working research draft**

## Research question

Can inquiry reviewers tell which claims came from original records and which were introduced by automated transcription, translation or summarisation?

## Starting evidence and candidate gap

Published defence inquiries provide examples of record-based review. The prevalence of AI-generated material in these cases remains to be established.

[Starting source](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/defence-safety-authority-dsa-service-inquiries-si)

## Proposed study

Insert known errors into synthetic translations and summaries, then test whether source-linked review finds them more reliably than ordinary review.

## Resume here

Search for two recent studies addressing this exact question. Log what they resolve, the population and period covered, and what remains testable. Revise the question if existing evidence already answers it. Record the source search date and selection criteria.
