## Research question

Can inquiry reviewers tell which claims came from original records and which were introduced by automated transcription, translation or summarisation?

## Starting evidence and candidate gap

Published defence inquiries provide examples of record-based review. The prevalence of AI-generated material in these cases remains to be established.
