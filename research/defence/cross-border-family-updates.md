# Cross-border family updates

**Drafted 16 November 2026 · Published work in progress**

Published work in progress. Research is underway. The questions, evidence and paper structure may change as the work develops.

## Research question

Who keeps families informed when a missing-person case spans multiple jurisdictions and the available records disagree?

## Starting evidence and candidate gap

The Second Geneva Convention supplies a wartime search-duty comparator. Establish applicable peacetime duties separately for each case.

[Starting source](https://ihl-databases.icrc.org/en/ihl-treaties/gcii-1949/article-18)

## Proposed study

Trace a small public case sample and record transfers of responsibility, contradictions and gaps in documented family updates.

## Resume here

Search for two recent studies addressing this exact question. Log what they resolve, the population and period covered, and what remains testable. Revise the question if existing evidence already answers it. Record the source search date and selection criteria.
