# Autonomous vessel oversight

**Drafted 16 November 2026 · Working research draft**

## Research question

What evidence makes remote operator and onboard software decisions independently reviewable after a civilian maritime incident?

## Starting evidence and candidate gap

Use the 2026 IMO MASS Code as a commercial comparator. Identify which evidence requirements apply to cargo ships and which questions remain open outside that scope.

[Starting source](https://www.imo.org/en/mediacentre/pressbriefings/pages/imo-adopts-mass-code.aspx)

## Proposed study

Compare documented decision records with synthetic cases involving conflicting operator and software accounts. Measure whether reviewers agree on who made each decision.

## Resume here

Search for two recent studies addressing this exact question. Log what they resolve, the population and period covered, and what remains testable. Revise the question if existing evidence already answers it. Record the source search date and selection criteria.
