Public identity

The directory keeps platform links separate from the main portfolio so the website does not become a second Linkme. Manifold Grace remains the explanatory source; social networks are distribution and discovery channels.

That rule applies to X, Vimeo, Facebook, YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat, SoundCloud, Spotify, ResearchGate, Medium and DEV where the exact public destination has not yet been confirmed.
