    What a research release contains

    A release fixes a reviewable version of the work: report, permitted code/data, figures, run instructions, evidence records, attribution, limitations and change notes. The public case study explains it; a commit/tag or archival record identifies the exact artefacts. Publication, independent review and scientific validation are separate statuses.

    • Check that the claims match the available evidence, including failures and contrary results.
    • State the version, environment, licence, contribution record and reproducibility/accessibility limits.
    • Add citation information. If an archival deposit is actually made, record its version-specific identifier.
    • Prepare any originator email or portal submission as a separate, reviewable step; record delivery, response and collaboration accurately.
