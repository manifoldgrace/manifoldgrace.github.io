Related technologies and terminology corrections

These are not counted as additional AI architectures or machine-learning algorithms. They may be relevant to project integration.

  • Blockchain — Distributed-ledger infrastructure, not AI architecture
  • Business process automation — Workflow/process engineering; can optionally integrate AI
  • Robotic process automation (RPA) — Software workflow automation, not physical robotics or AI by definition
  • Robotic programming with PTZ cameras — Robotics and perception system integration; PTZ cameras are hardware, NLP/CV are optional modules
  • Convoluted network — Terminology correction: convolutional neural network (CNN); covered by CNN feature hierarchy
  • Neural networks — Umbrella family; individual architectures separately listed

Research evidence and limits

The classification is an evidence-based working taxonomy, not a universal canonical count. A few system architectures can also be described as methods at a different level; the register assigns one primary home for counting and records that qualification. All proposed project and paper links remain working templates, not publications.
