Tracking the route from applicant to employee

A structured record of applications, interview stages, offers and outcomes can support evidence of genuine activity, identify where follow-up is warranted and help revisit appropriate employers over time. The accompanying tracker uses date filters, an optional ten-applications-per-day target, evidence references, downloadable backups and conversion statistics.

Why keep a record? Benefits, caveats & evidence quality

Tracking can prevent repeated submissions, preserve confirmation references and reveal where time is being spent. It also adds administration, can create misleading impressions from small samples and does not independently verify submission merely because an entry exists. Keep actual dated receipts or messages, and protect private application material.
