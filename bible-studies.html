Bible Studies

A structured study space for Scripture, theology and teaching. The aim is to preserve notes and developing interpretations while distinguishing personal study, denominational positions and questions that remain open.

STUDY STRUCTURE

Study corridors

Books of the BiblePassage-by-passage observations, themes and context.
DoctrineTrinity, salvation, Spirit, church, sacraments and eschatology.
FormationDiscipleship, vocation, character, work and spiritual practice.
Church historyTraditions, councils, movements and historical development.
Faith & public lifeJustice, stewardship, governance, technology and civic responsibility.
Questions under studyAreas where traditions differ or where my position is still developing.
TEACHING MEDIA

Videos & study images

Short teaching videos, photographed notes or diagrams can sit here with the passage, topic and date recorded. Media should support the study rather than replace the biblical text or the sources being discussed.

Where an interpretation is contested, the note should identify the major alternatives and explain why one reading currently appears stronger or weaker instead of presenting disagreement as though it does not exist.
