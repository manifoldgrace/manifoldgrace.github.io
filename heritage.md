## 02 — English corridor

### Biological and adoptive family history

My maternal heritage includes **English biological ancestry** and a **legal-adoptive family history through the Symondson family**.

Source: [Family archive and research record](archive.html)

- [Henry Symondson (1832–1911)](https://kimsimmonds.net/ps02/ps02_490.html) — musician, band sergeant and staff sergeant.
- [Henry John Symondson (1860–1926)](https://kimsimmonds.net/ps02/ps02_478.html) — musician with the Scots Guards.

- [John Danby (1757–1798)](https://kimsimmonds.net/ps03/ps03_148.html) — singer, organist and composer; also documented in the [Royal Society of Musicians archive](https://www.rsmgb.org/news/from-the-archives-royal-charter).

### Simmonds–Deakin and deeper collateral pedigree

The published genealogy traces the [Symondson/Simmonds → Deakin branch](https://kimsimmonds.net/charts/pedigree_kim_10.html), including **Alfred Henry Percy Simmonds and Margaret Patience Deakin**, and extends through **Beaufort-related collateral ancestry to John of Gaunt and Edward III**.

My grandmother's exact position within the wider Symondson network is **likely to become increasingly precise as the adoption and civil records are assembled**, and I am researching that connection further.

**English corridor conclusion:** the legal-adoptive and wider published family histories provide a substantial documented framework, with the precise connection between my grandmother's branch and the wider historical pedigree continuing to be refined.

## 03 — Evidence wording

- **Documented** — supported by a family primary record, legal record, archival catalogue, court record or other identifiable source.
- **Strongly supported** — several independent facts align around the same family, place or historical structure.
- **Likely / researching further** — the evidence points toward the connection and the precise linking record is being sought.

**Overall conclusion:** both heritage corridors now have a structured source trail. The strongest existing facts are stated directly, while likely connections are presented as active research that should become more precise as further family and archival records are recovered.
