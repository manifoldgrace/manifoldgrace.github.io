# Heritage

**Two family-history corridors, presented through linked sources and a clear evidence trail. Documented relationships are stated directly; likely connections are described as likely while research continues.**

## 01 — Ghanaian corridor

### Immediate family line

My paternal family is **Ga-Dangme from Accra**. The immediate family line preserved in the family archive is:

**Grace → father → Christopher Akai Nettey → Herbert Mills Nettey**

Christopher Akai Nettey died on **15 May 2023 aged 88**. His genealogical work on elders, descendants, wives, children and family branches remains a central source for the archive.

Source: [Family archive and research record](archive.html)

### Swalaba, Sonmenaa and Gbese

The wider association with **Swalaba, Sonmenaa and Gbese** is strongly supported by public reporting on the **Asafoatse C.B. Nettey Family of the Swalaba Quarter of Sonmenaa in Gbese**.

Source: [Ghana News Agency — Asafoatse C.B. Nettey Family](https://gna.org.gh/2024/11/we-will-protect-our-legacy-asafoatse-c-b-nettey-family/)

The exact generation-by-generation path connecting my documented line to earlier historical figures is **likely to be recoverable from surviving family and archival records**, and I am researching that path further.

### Earlier documented Nettey relationship

A 1936 West African Court of Appeal record identifies **Akai Kofi Nettey as the eldest surviving son of Nii Nettey Quarshie** in litigation involving Horse Road, Ussher Town.

Source: [1936 WACA record](https://www.judy.legal/case/c-brandford-nettey-judgment-debtor-v-the-gold-coast-independent-press-ltd-and-others-execution-creditors-appellants-and-akai-kofi-nettey-representing-the-children-of-nii-nettey-quarshie-deceased-claimant-respondent)

Herbert Mills Nettey's precise position relative to this branch is **likely to become clearer as the surviving family papers and court records are examined**.

My grandmother's exact position within the wider Symondson network is **likely to become increasingly precise as the adoption and civil records are assembled**, and I am researching that connection further.

**English corridor conclusion:** the legal-adoptive and wider published family histories provide a substantial documented framework, with the precise connection between my grandmother's branch and the wider historical pedigree continuing to be refined.

## 03 — Evidence wording

- **Documented** — supported by a family primary record, legal record, archival catalogue, court record or other identifiable source.
- **Strongly supported** — several independent facts align around the same family, place or historical structure.
- **Likely / researching further** — the evidence points toward the connection and the precise linking record is being sought.

**Overall conclusion:** both heritage corridors now have a structured source trail. The strongest existing facts are stated directly, while likely connections are presented as active research that should become more precise as further family and archival records are recovered.
