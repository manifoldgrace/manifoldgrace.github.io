**Status:** public-source desk review, **not an independent corporate forensic audit**.

## Conventions
- A verified **source statement** means the linked page contains the stated assertion; it does not prove all underlying corporate data independently.
- Statutory text must be read by jurisdiction, commencement, subsequent amendment or repeal, and historical date.
- No private correspondence, payslips, employment records, confidential company documents, or personal identifiers should be submitted to this public repository.
- Link each future derived claim to `source_id` and `claim_id`, record the review date and preserve contradictory or superseding material.
