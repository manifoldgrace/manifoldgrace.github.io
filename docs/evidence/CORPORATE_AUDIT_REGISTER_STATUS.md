## Scope and limitations

The research inputs propose **300 numbered entries across 20 broad sectors**. These are **research candidates**, not 300 independently audited companies. The list includes public authorities, technical products, subsidiaries, historical companies and operating divisions, as well as parent corporations.

An organisational homepage, generic statute or marketing statement does **not** verify a workforce demographic claim, internal communication chain, document retention period, misconduct allegation, or historical technical contribution.

**Current independent audit count: 0/300.** This counts completed individual source-and-claim audits for the supplied 300-row index; it does not mean no relevant documentary sources exist. Do not publish statuses such as “verified primary-source node” until each specific assertion has been checked.

These relationships require date-specific confirmation.
- UK public bodies and independent institutes are not automatically companies; the project is an **organisation and technology-ecosystem index**.
- The phrase “General Corporate Limitation Act 1980 (6 Years)” is incorrect as a universal record-retention requirement. The Limitation Act 1980 sets various limitation periods, not a blanket corporate document preservation duty.
- Assertions of majority-minority staffing, deliberate erasure, criminal destruction, or coordinated motives remain investigatory hypotheses unless supported by specific evidence.
- Original corporate URLs are research starting points, not per-claim audit references. Future case records should link to the dated underlying filings, statements, statutes or other specific sources.

## Minimum requirement for each organisation-specific audit

1. **Audit ID and entity:** exact historical/legal name, entity type, sector, jurisdiction and corporate family.
2. **Narrow claim:** precise event, contribution, product, employment trend or statutory duty; separate claims for each assertion.
3. **Source:** directly linked original report, filing, regulatory instrument, public project evidence or appropriately authorised source, including date and access conditions.
4. **Evidence appraisal:** supported / contradicted / disputed / insufficient evidence, with limitations and reviewer.
5. **Legal analysis:** applicable law, record category, effective date and jurisdiction; no automatic offence determination.
6. **Corrections:** version history, rationale, contradictory sources and source-access limitations.
7. **Accessibility and confidentiality:** do not solicit sensitive personal documents in public GitHub issues or static website forms.
