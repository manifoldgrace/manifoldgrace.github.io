# Escalation and review protocol — Design proposal

**Status: design only; not a live evidence upload, whistleblowing, regulatory reporting or automated referral service.**

## Intake boundaries

The public website may point to open-source documentation and an expression-of-interest route. It must not solicit confidential corporate data, personal payroll information, medical records, identity documents, case files, whistleblower disclosures or credentials through a public GitHub issue or a static GitHub Pages form.

## Safe future workflow

1. Display consent, permitted document types, privacy notice, retention schedule and safe alternative reporting routes.
2. Authenticate/authorise secure private intake where needed; run malware, content and access-control checks.
3. Preserve an original securely if lawfully received; generate checksum, receipt date and custody log.
4. Create a separately reviewed and appropriately redacted research extract where lawful.
5. Have a qualified reviewer assess provenance, statutory context, evidential uncertainty and risk.
6. Route only an authorised, minimal referral to an appropriate body; do not automatically transmit personal records.
7. Document acknowledgements, corrections and outcomes, including declined or unverified allegations.

**No automatic legal verdicts:** section 173 of the Data Protection Act 2018 and section 77 of the Freedom of Information Act 2000 require statutory elements, including context and intent. File absence or deletion alone does not establish criminality. Lawful retention schedules and data minimisation may require deletion.

**No universal fixed retention period:** classify documents by actual statutory purpose, jurisdiction, date and applicable rules, with human review.

## Possible recipients

UK ICO (personal-data rights), EHRC (equality), National Archives (public records policy), DSIT (AI policy), parliamentary committees (scrutiny), OSR (official statistics), HMRC NMW (specific wage concerns), Acas (employment guidance), NIST and international research bodies (technical policy). An organisation's mission does not imply that any advertised mailbox receives case reports.

Contact addresses must be checked on official pages at time of referral. Incident-reporting channels must be reserved for actual incidents. Track sent/not-sent status accurately.
