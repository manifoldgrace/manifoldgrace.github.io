Use the shared methodology guide to record the claim, baseline, strongest rival explanation, scope and ethics. Keep the research agenda and the methodology guide separately navigable. Before release, record the verified first-public date, archive each public version and document changes, contributors and AI assistance. Select a venue or funding route only if and when relevant.

Studies in development

Synthetic institutional records and false corroboration

AI evaluation · Study design in development

Paper or output. Methods and jurisprudence paper on evidentiary lineage Project. Build matched fictional housing appeals with dependent and genuinely independent corroboration; add silent transformation and confidentiality cases after the first scoring check. Evaluation. Compare chronological review, a source index and a decision evidence trail with identical source access. Score shared-origin detection, true-fact retention, contestability and time.

Meaningful review under confidentiality

AI evaluation · Study design in development

Paper or output. Jurisprudence paper on participation and independent scrutiny Project. Develop hard cases for gisting, restricted evidence and missing records. Check AF (No 3), Tariq and Osborn within their procedural contexts. Evaluation. Test whether each explanation lets a reviewer identify a material error and specify what the affected person could use to challenge it.

Institutional correction and appeals

AI evaluation · Study design in development

Paper or output. Public administration paper on review powers and outcomes Project. Develop a coding guide for comparable appeals, including rejected, unresolved and withdrawn cases. Evaluation. Compare AI-assisted and human coding of appeal upheld, record corrected and opportunity restored.

Administrative burden

AI evaluation · Study design in development

Paper or output. Qualitative social research paper on the work of challenging a decision Project. Design interviews and participant-led timelines that include people who never filed or stopped midway. Evaluation. Check whether AI-assisted coding preserves participant accounts, distinguishes evidence from inference and flags ambiguity.

Meaningful human oversight and defensive administration

AI evaluation · Study design in development

Paper or output. Governance paper on substantive review and procedural delay Project. Test protected time, access to original evidence, override routes and sign-off requirements on fictional decisions. Evaluation. Score justified corrections, missed errors, incorrect overrides and time, including whether additional sign-off adds value.

Authority and responsibility for automated decisions

AI evaluation · Study design in development

Paper or output. Doctrinal and institutional paper on delegated authority Project. Map instructions, vendor responsibilities, custody, actions and routes to redress in fictional allocations. Evaluation. Test whether AI can identify who authorised an action, which evidence supports attribution and where the record is incomplete.

Multi-principal alignment and normative uncertainty

AI evaluation · Study design in development

Paper or output. Normative and technical paper on competing duties Project. Build a bounded allocation simulator with stated rights, decision powers and escalation routes. Evaluation. Compare weighted preferences, firm constraints and explicit referral. Score violations, overrides and unnecessary referrals.

Provenance and changing archives

AI evaluation · Study design in development

Paper or output. Methods paper on source dependence in longitudinal records Project. Build a corpus with known lineage and vary copying, synthetic summaries and missing provenance. Evaluation. Measure recovery of known trends and false discounting of independent records.

Reproducibility and living research

AI evaluation · Study design in development

Paper or output. Methods paper on changing models and dated evidence Project. Preserve prompts, outputs, model metadata, scoring rules and research snapshots. Record protocol changes and corrections. Evaluation. Reproduce scores from preserved outputs, then rerun on available models and report whether the conclusion transfers.

Handover and institutional continuity

AI evaluation · Study design in development

Paper or output. Institutional design paper on maintaining a review method Project. Prepare a versioned protocol, scoring guide and training cases. Log reviewer questions and test handover to an independent team. Evaluation. Measure scoring agreement, ability to audit earlier findings and preservation of correction history.

Independent verification with restricted data

AI evaluation · Study design in development

Paper or output. Research governance paper on review without raw access Project. Prototype a governed query or a narrowly defined proof over fictional data. Document input authenticity and the scope of the verification separately. Evaluation. Voluntary commitments and remote verification

AI evaluation · Study design in development

Paper or output. Global AI governance paper on publicly checkable commitments Project. Trace selected Hiroshima AI Process claims to model-specific sources. Prototype a narrow remote evidence check using fictional logs. Evaluation. Score claim-to-source accuracy and distinguish self-report, corroboration and unverified or stale evidence.
