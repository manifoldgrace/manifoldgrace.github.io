LIVE EVENTS · COMMUNITY CONTRIBUTION

Priory Live — sustained volunteering

I volunteered with Priory Live in Orpington over several years, working alongside Nick Barclay and the wider event team. My involvement included planning meetings over the weeks surrounding events, local community support and artist liaison.

This contribution brought musicianship into contact with the practical work of live events: preparation, communication and supporting the people who make a community festival possible.

Planning context & future event research

Group planning discussions covered access, first aid, security and stewarding, ticketing, school and college participation, creative activities and event resources. These were shared planning discussions involving the wider team.

Alongside this involvement, I have researched mobile stage buses and trailers for potential future live-event projects.

RESEARCH × ARTISTIC PRACTICE

Intellectual roots, creative freedom

Social research, history, lived experience, politics, philosophy, news and the changing media environment can shape an artistic response. Music, painting, fashion and visual storytelling can also begin spontaneously, before their intellectual connections become clear.

This shared inquiry studies those connections without reducing art to argument or mistaking creative interpretation for verified fact.

Visual research & curation

Graceful Perspectives functions as a visual knowledge and practice archive: inspiration, professional-development references, books, techniques, Black history, landscape and nature, style, planning, future works and selected photographic material.

It is used less as a conventional social feed and more as a visual research system — a place to cluster references, preserve imagery, compare techniques and maintain continuity between ideas that may later become practical, creative or research work.
