Creative

Creative practice has been a continuous thread through my life and work, beginning with music and extending into sound, live production, visual research, photography, curation and creative technology.

CURRENT WORK

Music & sound

I am a saxophonist with training spanning classical foundations, jazz, church music and ensemble practice. Music led naturally into audio engineering, live sound, production and audiovisual technology, where performance and technical systems meet in real time.

Earlier creative work includes church music ministry, live events, performance and production environments. Selected historical material will be preserved through the Archive rather than presented as current activity.

REFERENCE

Visual research & curation

Graceful Perspectives functions as a visual knowledge and practice archive: inspiration, professional-development references, books, techniques, Black history, landscape and nature, style, planning, future works and selected photographic material.

It is used less as a conventional social feed and more as a visual research system — a place to cluster references, preserve imagery, compare techniques and maintain continuity between ideas that may later become practical, creative or research work.

ARCHIVE

Earlier creative material

Past performances, event material, recordings, production work and legacy creative websites are being selected for preservation. The aim is not to reproduce every past item, but to retain enough of the record to show continuity across music, technology and creative practice.

Archival selection will favour material that demonstrates development, collaboration, performance history or technical practice. Items that no longer represent current work will be dated and labelled clearly rather than silently removed or presented as contemporary.
