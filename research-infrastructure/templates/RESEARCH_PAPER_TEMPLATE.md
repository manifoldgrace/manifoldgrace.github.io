# Research Paper / Technical Report — reusable source template
> Template only. Do not submit as evidence of completed research.

## Title and contribution classification
Provisional topic, version, date, author(s), research programme and whether contribution is method, evidence, benchmark, theory or application.

## Abstract (structured)
Context · unresolved limitation · research question · method · result (only after measured) · contribution · limits.

## 1. Research question and null hypothesis
Define system, population, task, operational setting and precise falsifiable outcome. Distinguish expected findings from measured findings.

## 2. Strongest related research
| Year first published | Source/DOI | Claim and evidence | Method | Limitation relevant here | What our study changes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| YYYY | [verified] | | | | |

Include strongest counterevidence, replication attempts, similar projects and negative results. Search provenance: database, queries, date, inclusion/exclusion.

## 3. Architecture and materials
Versioned components, interfaces, model type, datasets, controlled conditions, permissions and dependency graph.

## 4. Method
Unit of analysis; comparison baseline; metrics; sample/power rationale; ethical consent; preregistered changes; threat model.

## 5. Claim-to-evidence matrix
| Claim ID | Claim | Evidence URL and locator | Source class | Contradictions | Status | Reviewer |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 6. Results
Only measured values with uncertainty intervals and reproducibility record; do not replace missing data with model-generated figures.

## 7. Ablations, falsification and alternative explanations
Counterfactuals, edge cases, adverse conditions, generalisation tests, sensitivity and confounders.

## 8. Discussion
What remains established, what is not established, applicability, broader implications and competing interpretations.

## 9. Limitations and governance
Data and model restrictions, bias, use constraints, compliance vs ethical considerations, human review.

## 10. Reproducibility and release
Repository commit, license, exact versions, scripts, environment, data availability, access date and correction channel.

## References and contribution statement
Use actual citation style and manuscript instructions required by chosen journal, conference or repository.
