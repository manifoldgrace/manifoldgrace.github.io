Procedural Justice Intelligence Lab

Proposed research and development programme · November 2026 · 12-month indicative roadmap

Objective: Develop and evaluate a provenance-aware, human-supervised AI decision-support system that identifies procedural failure modes, cumulative administrative burden, lost opportunities for correction and contradictions across multi-stage institutional decision chains.

Status: Concept, method and project outline prepared. No experimental findings, operational model, accredited standard, institutional partnership or published research paper are claimed.

Anonymised procedural chronology · Integrated Societal Intelligence roadmap · Contextual research question · Working paper initiative · Productivity record

1. Conceptual foundation

Investigate procedural closure propagation—a proposed, testable mechanism by which closure of an administrative procedure may be mistaken for substantive determination of all the underlying issues. Related phenomena include transferred administrative burden, issue-fragmentation, successive deadlines, evidence being received without examination, and remedy expiry. This mechanism is a research hypothesis, not an established novel discovery or finding of misconduct.

Ethical questions: Are procedures understandable and proportionate? Were material records evaluated? Which issues received a decision? Did a lawful correction remain possible? Who could intervene? Did the total process impose avoidable disadvantage?

4. Evaluation and falsification

Benchmark tasks: procedural closure versus substantive determination; lost-remedy identification; historical policy-version selection; evidence acknowledged versus reviewed; distinct issues versus duplicates; alternative correction routes; false-allegation resistance; privacy protection; and stability when document order changes.

6. Research integrity and governance

Use synthetic data by default. Any use of real records requires consent or other valid authority, secure access controls, minimisation, provenance, applicable legal review and clear restrictions on publication. Distinguish established facts from allegations, ethical judgments and legal determinations. Never convert a procedural concern into an automated allegation of unlawful conduct. Require independent human review for consequential conclusions, record counterexamples and preserve a correction log.

Existing work is not overwritten. This programme complements the anonymised chronology; the chronology is an exploratory source of questions, not an adjudicated case dataset or proof of AI-related wrongdoing.
