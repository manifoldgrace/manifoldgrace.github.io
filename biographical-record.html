UPBRINGING & EARLY FORMATION

Parents, service and early working life

Grace attributes much of her career direction and moral development to her parents, and to early service involving children and the church. Those settings reinforced a practical emphasis on responsibility, contribution, care for others and using whatever opportunities were available constructively.

As a child and young adult, she regularly spent afternoons gardening with her mother. Her father also helped create an early work-experience opportunity at Reuters in 2007, via Brook Street, on a SharePoint document-management security migration project that involved reporting and training engineers. Reuters later became part of Thomson Reuters; from that early experience, Grace continued to take contract work across London.

SELECTED WORKS & ARTEFACTS

A living index rather than a finished biography

Wikipedia biographies differ because lives differ: some are organised around offices held, others around publications, artistic works, public controversies, inventions, business activity or major life transitions. This page is designed to support that same flexibility without pretending that every possible heading is already necessary.

As the record grows, sections can be added, split or reordered according to actual evidence. A future substantial body of publications may justify a bibliography; a larger performance catalogue may justify a discography; a sequence of offices may justify a dedicated appointments section. Structure should follow the life rather than force the life into a template.

SOURCE STANDARD

How the record should be curated

Biographical statements should be supported according to what kind of claim they are. Publicly verifiable records are strongest for dates, offices, publications and formal achievements; first-person material is appropriate for intentions, personal interpretation and private experience; family history may require a mixture of documentary and oral evidence.

Where sources disagree, the page should not hide the disagreement. It can record the most probable interpretation based on the current source set and label the status as documented, highly probable, probable, plausible, unresolved or contradicted until stronger evidence changes the balance.
