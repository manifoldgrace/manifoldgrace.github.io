Independent Work & Impact

A record of the research, writing, building and civic contribution I continue beyond paid employment. This work has produced publications, digital projects, evidence trails and proposals for change.

What I have been working on

  1. Research and systems analysis

    Developing questions, evidence maps and interdisciplinary analyses across AI ethics, governance, institutions and public policy.

  2. Publications and intellectual property

    Writing books, developing original ideas and maintaining attributable publications and a record of authorship.

  3. Website and digital tools

    Building Manifold Grace, its research and learning frameworks, interactive tools and professional service demonstrations.

  4. Policy development and civic correspondence

    Turning observations into proposals, writing to MPs and institutions, and keeping questions open for review and accountability.

  5. Independent evidence and archive work

    Cross checking documents, dates and sources, maintaining provenance and investigating family and public history.

  6. Continuing learning and subject frameworks

    Organising a broad subject framework and pursuing structured reading and practical skill development.

  7. Professional services and venture development

    Developing skilled services and business concepts that connect technical capability with useful outcomes.

  8. Music and creative practice

    Continuing musicianship, creative work and the development of public facing cultural projects.

  9. Sport community and collaborative contribution

    Bringing teaching, mentoring and advisory experience into collaborative settings, with contributions documented where available.

  10. Impact and follow through

    Logging outputs, distinguishing proposals from delivered outcomes, and tracking the evidence of contribution and influence.

Outputs and contributions are recorded on their own terms. An email records a proposal or enquiry; it does not by itself establish that a policy changed or that a recipient adopted my work.
