Methodology / integrity

Designed to be challenged.

The twenty case labels are created by the author as synthetic reference answers. They are deliberately balanced across potential departures, justified decisions, incomplete evidence and completed corrections. This prevents the benchmark from rewarding an automatic presumption of institutional fault. These scenarios do not contain real case evidence, do not establish legal breaches and have not yet undergone independent professional validation.

In this first version, the browser scores a reader's classifications against published author labels; no live AI model is being trained or evaluated. The next experiment will independently review the reference key, then compare model predictions under an explicit, reproducible protocol. Scores and answers remain on this page until exported; no account or submission is needed.
