    SECURE DOCUMENT WORKFLOW

    Collaborate from different places

    Research collaborators can work on the same document from their own devices. The document provider manages sign-in, editing rights, comments and version history; this public catalogue is the research index, not a password-protected editor.

    01 · CHOOSESelect an area and research question

    Copy the structured outline and adapt the question, scope, evidence plan and roles together.

    02 · SHAREUse named email invitations

    Share the Google Doc or Word file with specific collaborators. Choose Viewer, Commenter or Editor permissions; sign in with account-level multi-factor authentication.

    03 · PRESERVERetain a trustworthy record

    Use document version history and named revisions. Send milestone summaries by email; periodically export independent snapshots to approved storage. Email copies complement, but do not replace, document backups.

    About four-digit codes, locking and automated updates

    A four-digit PIN is not a safe access-control mechanism for confidential collaborative research. Use identity-based permissions, strong sign-in and two-factor authentication instead. Automatic email-to-document ingestion, secure website editing, and category publication require an authenticated backend, an approved email ingestion service and document-provider API permissions. Those services are not connected by this public page; new categories are suggested for review rather than silently published.
