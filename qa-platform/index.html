    Research and product boundaries

    The product extends the existing continuous agent-assisted assurance research and modular architecture. The QA methods remain research proposals until validated. Product functionality is separately evaluated from methodological claims.

    Before live service launch: activate authentication and data controls; validate user isolation; configure privacy notice, data retention and recovery; implement the independent evaluation runner; run security, accessibility and regression tests; document the operating organisation and support routes.
