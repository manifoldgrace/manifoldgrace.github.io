AI Tool Security and Trust: 20 Documented Case Studies

Comparative evidence for assessing external AI models, research platforms, hosting providers, public code, automation services and third-party connections. Updated 10 November 2026. Cases include AI-specific systems and wider technology dependencies that an AI system may use.

Interpretation: An incident demonstrates a type of failure, not that every employee or service in the named organisation acts maliciously. Distinguish confirmed incidents, research findings, allegations and regulatory conclusions. Historic security failures do not establish present insecurity; an absence of known incidents is not proof of safety. Nationality or jurisdiction alone is not evidence of intent.

Risk categories for any AI integration

Data and intellectual property

Retention, provider training, contractor access, deletion, licences, copyright, transcripts and metadata.

Cybersecurity

Access controls, API keys, dependencies, update integrity, vulnerabilities, prompt injection, model execution and incident response.

Governance and geopolitical exposure

Applicable law, processing location, lawful disclosure requirements, ownership changes, service continuity and documented external dependencies.

Human and public-interest risks

Unfair or misleading outputs, surveillance, personal-data misuse, concentration of infrastructure and inadequate avenues to challenge decisions.

Evidence required before connecting any tool

Data custody and disclosure

Record processing and backup locations, retention windows, deletion controls, permitted model-training uses, subprocessors, support access, lawful requests and the rights of people whose data is included. Obtain a documented legal basis where personal data is processed.

Security architecture and supply chain

Record the software and model licence, repository source and maintainer, pinned release, software bill of materials where applicable, isolation of secrets, authentication method, audit events, vulnerability reporting and incident notification terms. Local open-source software still requires review.

Geopolitical and corporate dependencies

Record public corporate ownership, hosting and processing jurisdictions, relevant legal obligations, known material service restrictions and available exit paths. Assess actual policy and technical evidence, not assumptions about ethnicity, citizenship or personal political beliefs.

AI output safety and intellectual provenance

Test source accuracy, hallucination, independent corroboration, model-generated misinformation, prompt injection, improper retrieval, idea attribution and the reliability of recorded corrections. Keep human approval for consequential communications or publishing.

Decision record and reassessment

For each trial record who approved it, what data classes may be sent, what was tested, what failed, total expected cost, revocation steps and the next review date. A free tier is a pricing decision, not a security certification.
