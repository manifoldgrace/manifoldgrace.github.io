Concept and status

Hypothesis. Physical isolation can increase the need for local discretion while making outside review harder. Publication gaps can reflect reporting, access or disclosure practices. Compare comparable cases before attributing differences to a service or country.

Service, authority and conscience

Research question. How do published entry terms, delegated responsibilities and confidential advice routes vary by service and entry route?

Proposed test. Compare public recruitment and governance documents. Record oath, affirmation and commission separately. Test whether a person can obtain confidential advice outside immediate supervision.

Isolation and independent review

Research question. Does physical isolation delay independent access to records and witnesses after serious incidents?

Proposed test. Match public sea, land and air inquiries by severity and reporting rules. Track record custody, review access and verified corrective actions.

Jurisdiction and state responsibility

Research question. Who can establish facts or require a remedy when the legal zone, vessel status or attribution to a state is disputed?

Proposed test. Map the legal zone, the activity, each party’s stated position and the reviewer’s authority. Apply the same evidence standard across countries.

Interstate escalation

Research question. Would an automatic pause after a serious disputed encounter reduce retaliatory escalation?

Proposed test. Compare documented incidents with different outcomes. Test a proposed duty to contact counterparts, preserve evidence and initiate rapid review, with a time-limited pause on discretionary retaliation and exceptions for rescue and immediate threats.

Reporting and reprisals

Research question. Can personnel report harm outside immediate command and obtain review of alleged reprisals?

Proposed test. Trace protected channels, independent review and remedies. Treat reassignment or career delay as a signal for review, subject to alternative explanations.

Missing personnel and duties to families

Research question. When recovery is impossible, what can investigators establish and what information and support reach families?

Proposed test. Examine published records for notification, regular updates, explanation of unresolved facts and access to bereavement or pastoral support of the family’s choosing.
