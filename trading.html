STUDY FRAMEWORK

Read the market in layers

01 · REGIMETrend first

Start with the major indices across daily, weekly and intraday views. Ask whether price is trending, ranging or reversing before interpreting individual names.

02 · BREADTHLeadership & participation

Check whether gains are broad or concentrated. Compare large-cap leadership with weaker sectors and note whether several indices confirm the same direction.

03 · VOLATILITYRisk conditions

Watch volatility alongside index direction. A rising market with rising volatility tells a different story from a calm advance.

04 · RATES & FXMoney and discount rates

Track bond yields and the dollar because changing rates and currency strength can reprice equities, commodities and global risk assets.

05 · CROSS-ASSETConfirmation

Compare gold, oil, currencies and crypto with equity moves. Agreement across assets can strengthen an interpretation; divergence is a reason to investigate further.

06 · CATALYSTSCalendar before narrative

Check scheduled economic releases and central-bank events before deciding that a price move has one obvious cause.

This page is an educational monitoring tool. It does not execute trades, hold funds or provide personalised investment advice. Market data can be delayed, incomplete or subject to exchange and provider rules.
