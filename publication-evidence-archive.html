Downloadable Public Evidence Archive

Implemented as an open educational demonstration — 13 November 2026. Explore Manifold Grace's own dated public productivity-log entries through linked PDFs grouped by day, week, month and year, plus an all-records bundle. The same underlying index is available as CSV and JSON for research or machine-assisted analysis.

Scope and chronology: archive entries use the date on the public productivity log, not the original publication date of an externally cited article. External content is linked, not reproduced. A catalogue of available records cannot establish that all past publications exist or have been recovered.

Academic context and transparent reuse

The research and policy documentation explains the architecture, original code provenance, alternative paper types, higher-education use, accessibility and legal limits. The existing project register links beginner through advanced implementation pathways.

PDF outputs are intended for convenient download, reading, printing and reference, but PDF/UA accessibility conformance has not been certified. For assistive technologies and automated analysis, use the HTML directory and machine-readable JSON/CSV. No copyrighted source-article bodies or paywalled content are bundled.
