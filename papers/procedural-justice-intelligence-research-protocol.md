# Procedural Justice Intelligence Lab — Working Paper Initiative
**Manifold Grace · November 2026 · Status: research protocol and paper initiatives proposed; no experiments completed or academic publication claimed.**

## Paper 1: When Procedures Defeat Their Purpose
*Subtitle: Cumulative Administrative Harm and Contestable AI.*

### Abstract (proposal)
Study whether successive procedural decisions can cumulatively prevent examination or correction of an underlying administrative problem. Define and test "procedural closure propagation" as the risk that a procedural closure is treated as a merits determination in later decisions. Evaluate a provenance-aware AI decision-support framework against standard language-model baselines using synthetic administrative cases and human-coded issue-disposition ground truth.

## Paper 2: The Administrative Burden Paradox
*Subtitle: Institutional Complexity and Corrective Justice.*

### Abstract (proposal)
Investigate when multiple routes, rules and deadline dependencies transfer an excessive administrative load onto service users, and whether apparently individually compliant decisions produce preventable overall disadvantage. Test costs of learning, compliance, and psychological burden through simulated administrative pathways, while measuring fairness, remedy preservation and incorrect-accusation risk.

## Research questions
1. Can models reliably distinguish procedural closure from issue-level merits determination?
2. Do provenance-aware methods detect lost remedies better than ordinary prompting?
3. When do multiple individually compliant deadlines produce cumulative barriers?
4. Can models identify an available, legally grounded corrective pathway without inventing entitlements?
5. Does added complexity increase unsupported misconduct claims or review burden?

## Methods
- Evidence gap and originality check, including administrative burden, legal contestability, procedural justice, fairness, and institutional governance.
- Version-controlled policy retrieval, citation validation and provenance graphs.
- Synthetic cases covering justified refusals, genuine errors, and indeterminate outcomes.
- Independent double-coded ground truth with disagreement adjudication.
- Baselines: standard prompting, document retrieval, and provenance-enhanced decision support.
- Metrics: accuracy, remedy recall, precision, citation validity, calibration, re-identification risk and human-review effort.
- Ablation, counterexample and adversarial tests. Publish null results and errors.

## Ethics and safeguards
No identifiable case records, medical information, institutional names, confidential correspondence or privileged legal advice are included. Human oversight is mandatory. The working hypotheses do not constitute a finding of unlawful conduct or evidence that AI made any original decision.
