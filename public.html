Public

The public-facing part of Manifold Grace gathers conversations, speaking, selected life updates and social channels without turning the whole site into a social feed. It is designed for people who want a quicker, more human route through the site.

PUBLIC RECORD

Ways into the public-facing material

Different public formats serve different purposes. Live-session excerpts preserve spontaneous discussion; public speaking presents more prepared communication; life notes show selected context around routines, training, travel, hobbies and creative or academic development.

The separation is intentional. A debate clip should not be mistaken for a formal paper, and a personal photograph should not carry the same evidential weight as a research output. Manifold Grace keeps those registers connected but distinct.

Public without overexposure

I want the site to be relatable without becoming a complete record of private life. Public material is therefore selected for relevance, context or continuity rather than posted simply because it exists.

Real-time location, home details and unnecessary personal identifiers are not part of the design. Travel and day-in-the-life material can be shared retrospectively, keeping the human dimension of the site without turning it into live tracking.
