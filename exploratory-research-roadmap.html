Candidate studies and pilots

Agent reliability, reasoning faithfulness and synthetic training

AI evaluation · Study design in development

Paper or output. Technical paper candidates Project. Run separate controlled agent, reasoning-intervention and recursive-training experiments. Evaluation. Score error recovery, answer changes under matched interventions and rare-case retention.

Scarcity framing and network spillovers

AI evaluation · Study design in development

Paper or output. Behavioural economics and econometrics paper candidates Project. Design the framing experiment and a separate simulated network-policy comparison. Evaluation. Check whether AI-inferred links alter effect estimates and whether task wording changes measured beliefs.

Algorithmic pricing and endogenous measurement

AI evaluation · Study design in development

Paper or output. Industrial organisation and measurement paper candidates Project. Use simulated pricing panels and performance metrics with known generating mechanisms. Evaluation. Measure false positives under common shocks and whether a higher metric score masks worse outcomes.

AI adoption and early-career skill formation

AI evaluation · Study design in development

Paper or output. Labour economics paper candidate Project. Map junior tasks to learning opportunities in one profession. Compare unaided and supervised AI-assisted exercises. Evaluation. Assess later unassisted performance. Treat historical adoption and long-run employment effects as separate evidence questions.

Private compute infrastructure and cross-border contracting

AI evaluation · Study design in development

Paper or output. Global governance and commercial law paper candidates Project. Trace one public compliance rule through provider records and build fictional automated transactions with varying authorisations. Evaluation. Distinguish documented conduct from inferred motives and separate execution location from authorisation.

Disruption attribution and system-wide shocks

AI evaluation · Study design in development

Paper or output. Governance and methods paper candidates Project. Compare public post-incident findings and retrospective counterfactual designs using published or fictional records. Evaluation. Check timelines, alternative explanations and unsupported attribution. Keep the work focused on evidence assessment after an incident.

Local heat adaptation and social value procurement

AI evaluation · Study design in development

Paper or output. Climate governance and procurement paper candidates Project. Trace one heat commitment per borough and digital skills pledges in comparable contracts. Evaluation. Check claims against records of commitment, delivery, reach and outcomes.

Managed retreat, compound climate risk and fiscal stress

AI evaluation · Study design in development

Paper or output. Climate finance and governance paper candidates Project. Model one jurisdiction and one route from physical loss to fiscal exposure. Compare one-way and coupled scenarios. Evaluation. Verify extracted assumptions, test sensitivities and distinguish simulated results from observed impacts.

Transboundary climate intervention governance

AI evaluation · Study design in development

Paper or output. Climate governance and legal theory paper candidate Project. Use published assessments to compare evidentiary thresholds for investigation, precaution and compensation. Evaluation. Score how accurately AI conveys uncertainty and separates modelled effects, event attribution and legal responsibility.

Audit costs and market entry

AI evaluation · Study design in development

Paper or output. Regulatory economics paper candidate Project. Compare fixed costs and detection coverage under uniform and risk-tiered requirements. Evaluation. Use matched fictional audit cases and score errors detected, reviewer time and estimated compliance costs.

Environmental credit integrity

AI evaluation · Study design in development

Paper or output. Environmental measurement paper candidate Project. Study one credit type with differing baseline, measurement and durability assumptions. Evaluation. Check whether AI distinguishes observed physical change from evidence of additionality.

Emergency authority and democratic review

AI evaluation · Study design in development

Paper or output. Political science and institutional design paper candidate Project. Compare fixed expiry, renewal and retrospective review in fictional emergency decisions. Evaluation. Test authority reconstruction, missing reasons and renewal flags.

Automated monitoring offers and pilot

Venture pilot · Proposed

Paper or output. Venture development Project. Use the existing 50-idea Venture catalogue. Develop a four-week digital skills tender pilot from a retrospective sample; retain parliamentary and permit monitoring as alternatives. Evaluation. Check source coverage, matching and correction rates, owner review time and willingness to pay.
