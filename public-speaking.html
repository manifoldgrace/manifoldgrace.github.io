Editorial standard

A public-speaking extract should represent the point actually made rather than manufacture a stronger claim by cutting away essential context. Where a talk includes evidence or contested claims, related sources can be linked from the caption or a connected case study.

This keeps the page useful across corporate, academic, civic and creative settings without forcing every appearance into the same tone.
