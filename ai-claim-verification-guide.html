How to check AI-generated claims for accuracy

AI-generated answers can sound certain even when their evidence is incomplete. This practical guide introduces a repeatable method for checking factual assertions without assuming that fluent language proves correctness.

Scope: Informational research guide. Adapt the method to the relevant domain; follow applicable professional, legal and safeguarding requirements.

1. Identify the specific claim

Separate a response into testable claims: names, dates, figures, quotations, causation and recommendations. State the claim exactly enough that a source could support or contradict it.

2. Find independent primary evidence

Prefer original legislation, official publications, research papers, source datasets and contemporaneous records where relevant. A model-generated citation is a lead to check, not proof that the cited source exists or supports the statement.

3. Check source-to-claim coverage

For each claim, record the source URL, precise passage or data field, publication date and whether the source directly supports it, only partially supports it, or fails to address it. Distinguish an author’s inference from a source’s finding.

4. Evaluate chronology and corrections

Check whether a source was current at the date relevant to the question. Look for retractions, amended data, version histories and changes in institutional responsibility. Do not replace historical facts with present-day versions without explanation.

5. Test contradictions and uncertainty

Compare competing evidence; distinguish genuine disagreement from differences in definition, jurisdiction, timing or measurement. Record unresolved gaps rather than choosing a convenient version.

6. Report a calibrated conclusion

Classify claims as supported, partly supported, disputed, unsupported or unverified, with a reason. A claim that cannot be verified is not automatically false.

Worked reasoning example

Example: If an answer claims that a policy took effect on a particular date, locate the operative primary source, check commencement provisions, distinguish announcement from implementation, and report any qualification.
