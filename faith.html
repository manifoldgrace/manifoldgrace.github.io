Faith

Christian faith has been a longstanding part of my life, shaping my understanding of service, formation, responsibility, creativity and public life. This page records selected areas of study and reflection without turning a personal faith journey into a complete doctrinal statement.

FORMATION

Theology & Christian formation

My theological interests include Scripture, Christian history, discipleship, ministry, church formation and vocation. Church attendance was a consistent part of my childhood, teenage years and twenties; periods away at university and, later, the pandemic interrupted that continuity.

Church music and service formed part of my early creative life, including saxophone within ministry contexts, while theological study continues alongside wider academic and professional interests. I am especially interested in how belief becomes practice: character, responsibility, work, leadership, service and the treatment of other people.

ACTIVE INQUIRY

Faith, technology & public life

I explore how Christian thought engages questions of technology, work, education, governance, stewardship, justice and human flourishing without reducing complex public questions to slogans.

This overlaps naturally with systems research: values influence institutions, institutions influence technology, and technology can amplify or constrain the choices people are able to make.
