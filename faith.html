Faith

Christian faith has been a longstanding part of my life, shaping my understanding of service, formation, responsibility, creativity and public life. This page records selected areas of study and reflection without attempting to turn a personal faith journey into a complete doctrinal statement.

FORMATION

Theology & Christian formation

My theological interests include Scripture, Christian history, discipleship, ministry, church formation and vocation. Church music and service formed part of my early creative life, while theological study continues alongside wider academic and professional interests.

I am especially interested in how belief becomes practice: character, responsibility, work, leadership, service and the treatment of other people.

ACTIVE INQUIRY

Faith, technology & public life

I explore how Christian thought engages questions of technology, work, education, governance, stewardship, justice and human flourishing without reducing complex public questions to slogans.

This overlaps naturally with my research in AI ethics and institutional responsibility: what people build, what institutions permit, who bears consequences, and what responsible use of power looks like.

SELECTED REFLECTION

Areas being developed

This part of the site will grow through selected reflections rather than frequent devotional posting. The emphasis is on areas where theology intersects with formation, vocation, ethics, culture and public responsibility.

Positions may develop as study continues. Where Christians or traditions disagree, the page will distinguish settled personal convictions from questions still being examined, and will avoid presenting contested interpretations as universally agreed doctrine.

  • Biblical study and discipleship
  • Faith, vocation and work
  • Christian ethics and technology
  • Church, community and civic responsibility
  • Music, worship and formation
