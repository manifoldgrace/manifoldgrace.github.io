Research question

What cascades — and what lasts?

How can a coherent public knowledge identity be structured to maximise relevant discovery and useful secondary connections while preserving authorship, provenance, interpretability and independent verification?

Original work → Persistent record → Connected profiles → Independent references → Qualified discovery → Long-term use

Distribution is not the same as validation. A self-authored profile establishes what its author says; credible external references, verifiable professional evidence and independently evaluated publications contribute different forms of corroboration.

What should be published with any reported number?

Reporting period; collection date; dataset scope; numerator and denominator; measurement tool; inclusion and exclusion rules; known missing data; and whether the number is observed, estimated, modelled or independently verified.

Probability and network effects

Model stochastic cascades

A simplified branching model estimates how many subsequent relevant discoveries may follow from an initial exposure. It is for comparing assumptions, not predicting platform algorithms, rankings or personal success.

Expected next-generation discoveries = current relevant discoveries × average onward shares × probability of a relevant follow-on discovery
Reproduction factor0.24
Additional discoveries expected31
Total across generations131

Illustrative expected values only. Formula: R = onward shares × relevant discovery probability; expected total = initial discoveries × (1 + R + … + R^g). Assumes constant parameters, independent propagation and no audience overlap or saturation. These assumptions seldom hold perfectly in real networks.

Choose actions by expected value — not visibility alone

Indicative decision score = probability of relevant reach × value of outcome × persistence × onward-reference potential ÷ time and cost

This heuristic has no universal calibrated weights. Compare scenarios against observed outcomes, estimate uncertainty ranges and revisit after each reporting period. Where evidence allows, use controlled comparisons or attribution analysis rather than assuming every new enquiry came from a single post.

Publishing architecture

High-value durable content forms

Evidence-based case studies

Document problem, work performed, artefacts, verification status and outcome. Useful for employers and collaborators.

Research papers and data

Persistent identifiers, methods, versioning, sources and appropriate rights statements support citation and reuse.

Reference explainers

Glossaries, tutorials, diagrams and policy briefs connect specialist work to broader audiences.

Author and project records

Stable biographies, structured metadata, project pages and reciprocal official links help readers navigate the identity network.

Publication cascade: primary work → canonical landing page → professional summary → visual explainer → scholarly or professional discussion → verifiable external references. Each derivative should point to the primary source and disclose substantive changes.

Research protocol

Build a baseline, then test

  1. Inventory: record publicly intended profiles, URLs, identifiers and core authored works.
  2. Baseline: measure indexing, referrals, citations, accessible pages and meaningful enquiries for a defined period.
  3. Graph: map identity → works → organisations → independent references; mark evidence and uncertainty for each edge.
  4. Intervene: improve one category at a time — e.g. metadata, original publication, collaboration or distribution.
  5. Compare: track changes over consistent windows and account for seasonality, platform changes and confounding events.
  6. Report: publish methodology and a dated table, including blanks where data are not yet available.

Current measurement status: no analytics accounts or scholarly APIs are connected to this page. No impressions, citations, conversion rates, centrality scores or probabilities are presented as observed results. The simulator is local and does not collect user data.
