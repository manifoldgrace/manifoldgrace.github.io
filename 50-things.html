METHOD

Why I frame it this way

This is not intended as a comparison with somebody else's timetable. It is a record of choices, experiences and work I could meaningfully influence, separated from structural conditions, timing, bureaucracy or circumstances that were not fully mine to control.

The title allows the list to reach fifty over time, but I would rather publish fewer truthful lines than manufacture achievements simply to complete a number.
