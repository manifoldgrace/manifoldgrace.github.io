Case Studies

Case studies translate broad interests into bounded problems. They are grouped by research area and labelled by status so published work, practice-based examples and developing inquiries remain distinguishable.

01 · AI ethics, governance & provenance
CURRENT INQUIRY

01.1 · Provenance in AI-mediated knowledge

How source discovery, curation, human contribution and attribution can remain visible when research passes through automated systems.

The case examines both technical and governance questions: what should be recorded, who receives credit, and how a later reader can reconstruct the chain from original source to machine-assisted synthesis.

IN DEVELOPMENT

01.2 · AI infrastructure as a governance layer

Cloud, model, data and deployment choices can become ethical choices when they determine latency, access, observability, resilience and who controls the system.

This case links engineering architecture to institutional responsibility rather than treating infrastructure as politically or ethically neutral.

02 · Sustainable infrastructure
PUBLISHED

02.1 · London Smart Piezoelectric Flooring Pilot

A proposed London pilot combining kinetic energy harvesting, AI-optimised infrastructure and public-space design, published on Zenodo.

The case joins engineering feasibility with accessibility, ESG, public engagement, governance and urban systems rather than treating renewable technology as an isolated device problem.

03 · Institutions, law & public administration
IN DEVELOPMENT

03.1 · Procedure, accountability & administrative systems

A research frame for examining how rules, discretion, records, appeals and fragmented responsibility affect people navigating institutions.

The emphasis is comparative and systemic: distinguishing an individual dispute from the wider design features that make errors easier or harder to detect and correct.

IN DEVELOPMENT

03.2 · Accessibility and participation in public systems

How transport, education, welfare and other public systems translate formal rights into practical access.

The case is intended to compare policy promises, operational rules and lived usability without assuming that legal entitlement automatically produces accessible service.

04 · Technical and professional systems
PRACTICE-BASED

04.1 · AV reliability, latency & intelligent tracking

Professional audiovisual environments provide a concrete way to study reliability, failure modes, automation, human oversight and the difference between nominal specification and real performance.

Future write-ups can break individual architectures into signal flow, network dependencies, monitoring, break/fix reasoning and ethical questions around automated capture or tracking.

IN DEVELOPMENT

04.2 · Cloud and DevOps architecture walkthroughs

Small infrastructure builds can be used as transparent case studies for networking, deployment, logging, security and resilience.

These cases will favour explained diagrams and decisions over screenshots of tools, so the reader can understand why a design works and what trade-offs it creates.

05 · Heritage, archives & historical method
ACTIVE

05.1 · Two-corridor genealogy and evidence grading

The Heritage project compares Ghanaian Ga-Dangme family research with English biological and adoptive-family records while keeping biological descent, legal adoption, collateral connection and community belonging distinct.

It also tests an evidence scale—documented, highly probable, probable, plausible, unresolved or contradicted—so incomplete records are neither exaggerated nor discarded prematurely.

Case-study standard

Each case should identify the question, evidence base, stakeholders, technical or institutional mechanism, competing interpretations and current status. Where a conclusion is disputed, the page should describe why one interpretation is more or less likely on the current evidence rather than disguising uncertainty.

Longer papers, diagrams, code, media or source registers can then sit behind the case summary without turning this landing page into a wall of text.
