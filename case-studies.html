05 · Heritage, archives & historical method
ACTIVE

05.1 · Two-corridor genealogy and evidence grading

The Heritage project compares Ghanaian Ga-Dangme family research with English biological and adoptive-family records while keeping biological descent, legal adoption, collateral connection and community belonging distinct.

It also tests an evidence scale—documented, highly probable, probable, plausible, unresolved or contradicted—so incomplete records are neither exaggerated nor discarded prematurely.

Case-study standard

Each case should identify the question, evidence base, stakeholders, technical or institutional mechanism, competing interpretations and current status. Where a conclusion is disputed, the page should describe why one interpretation is more or less likely on the current evidence rather than disguising uncertainty.

Longer papers, diagrams, code, media or source registers can then sit behind the case summary without turning this landing page into a wall of text.
