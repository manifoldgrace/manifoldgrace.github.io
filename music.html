Music

Music is both an artistic practice and part of my technical history. This page separates performance, musicianship and future composition work from the broader Creative landing page so each can develop with its own evidence and explanations.

PRACTICE

Saxophone & musicianship

My musical background includes classical foundations, jazz, church music and ensemble practice, with saxophone as the clearest instrumental through-line. The point of this page is not to force that background into one genre identity, but to document technique, repertoire, performance environments and the way musicianship has influenced later technical work.

Photographs, performance extracts and practice notes can be added selectively where they demonstrate progression or context rather than simply filling a gallery.

IN DEVELOPMENT

Composition & musical language

Composition is a future area for more deliberate development. I am interested in a refined language that can draw from classical and jazz traditions without pretending that eclectic listening and other genre influences have to be abandoned in order to become more sophisticated.

The longer-term aim is fluidity with discipline: allowing taste to mature, learning the formal tools required to write convincingly, and then letting different genre corridors inform rather than confine the work.
