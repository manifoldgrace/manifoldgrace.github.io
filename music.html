Music

Music is both an artistic practice and part of my technical history. This page separates performance, musicianship and composition from the broader Creative landing page so each can develop with its own evidence and explanations.

PRACTICE

Saxophone & musicianship

My musical background includes classical foundations, jazz, church music and ensemble practice, with saxophone as the clearest instrumental through-line. The point of this page is not to force that background into one genre identity, but to document technique, repertoire, performance environments and the way musicianship has influenced later technical work.

Photographs, performance extracts and practice notes can be added selectively where they demonstrate progression or context rather than simply filling a gallery.

WORK IN PROGRESS

Composition & musical language

My creative work includes compositions and projects held privately while recording and mixdowns are completed. Classical training, jazz and wider listening inform a musical language that allows discipline and creative freedom to coexist.

Publication is a separate stage of the work. Instrument maintenance, recording equipment and studio access have affected that stage, alongside other professional and study commitments. I am preparing work for release at a standard that reflects my training and artistic intentions.
