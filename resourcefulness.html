SEQUENCE

Optimisation order

  1. Stabilise the primary base.
  2. Monetise existing skills.
  3. Turn knowledge into reusable intellectual property.
  4. Build repeatable media and licensing assets.
  5. Add product commerce.
  6. Allocate surplus capital to savings and investments.
  7. Acquire or monetise property and other assets.
  8. Consolidate mature streams into scalable enterprise ownership.

Working principle: establish, systematise, review, then add the next stream.
