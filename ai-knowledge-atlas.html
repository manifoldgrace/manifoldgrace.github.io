AI Knowledge Atlas: Foundations, Techniques, Systems and Applications

Nested, expandable research map that separates disciplines, scientific techniques, technical systems, operational environments, governance and application domains. The directory is designed for breadth and progressive source validation, not as a claim that the whole field has already been exhaustively documented.

Research continuity: This atlas organises longstanding study of AI, computing and technical systems; it does not imply these subjects have only recently been undertaken. The 2017 early-years programming and robotics work is separately recorded in the systems atlas.
