Initial literature check · 15 November 2026. Source dependence and text reuse are established research areas. The remaining candidate is a comparative test on administrative bundles with missing lineage that scores both false grouping and unwarranted claims of independence, separately from factual correction.

Dong et al. (2009), source dependence · Leskovec et al. (2009), phrase propagation · W3C PROV (2013) · Evidence judged convincing by language models (2024) · GRACE (2026)

Intelligence and audit literature check · 15 November 2026. UK intelligence doctrine explicitly warns about circular reporting. Analysis of Competing Hypotheses compares how evidence bears on rival explanations, and audit standard AS 1105 distinguishes direct and independent evidence from indirect evidence and copies. Dong et al. (2009, §2) explicitly define dependence to include derivation from a common source, while restricting their tractable detection model to direct copying. The ACL 2024 ConflictingQA paper studies which evidence models find convincing; it supplies adjacent work rather than a direct test of shared-origin repetition.

Candidate gap retained. The sources checked did not establish a matched evaluation on small administrative bundles with selectively missing source links. Compare independent observations of the same event, reports drawing on one feed and derivative echoes with a hidden root. Include an independent observation of a different event as a control. Score false independence, false grouping and justified uncertainty separately from factual correction. Use a simple source index and a competing-hypotheses matrix as comparators.

Dong et al. (2009), §2 · UK Joint Doctrine Publication 2-00 · Heuer, Analysis of Competing Hypotheses · PCAOB AS 1105 · ConflictingQA (2024)
