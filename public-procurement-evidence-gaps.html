What delivery did the buyer verify for Wave 6, Lot 3, and what did it pay?

Open · Public sources checked · OCID ocds-h6vhtk-0544a3

The award and £120,000 contract value are documented. The public records reviewed leave actual payments and lot-level outcomes unverified. Each open question below identifies a contribution that could advance the record.

Delivery and the applicable target

Open gap. The operative baseline, qualifying-start denominator and verified completions, interviews and outcomes. What would resolve it. Signed schedules, relevant variations and a dated buyer-validated aggregate for this lot. A writer can locate the records or reconcile the definitions and reporting periods.

Payment and record custody

Open gap. Actual payments linked to approved claims. What would resolve it. Payment records carrying the lot reference, milestone, amount and date, or a documented account of which entity holds them. The council search only covers the files reviewed.

Wider research questions

Can a public audit distinguish unavailable records from records never created? Can evidence coverage survive a move across languages, jurisdictions and publication systems? Can source-lineage methods flag shared observations without discounting independent evidence? These are candidate research gaps. Establish what existing research covers before claiming novelty. AI evaluation · Study design in development

Give a model the dated source bundle. Score attribution, disclosure-rule applicability, search limits and the next evidence needed on a 0–2 scale each. Log contract-value-as-payment and cross-wave attribution errors separately. Use a separate 0–2 follow-up score for updating one claim when a fictional partial disclosure arrives. The written calibration examples are design material. Live model performance remains to be measured.
