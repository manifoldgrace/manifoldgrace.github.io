Method, evidence and limitations

Collection reads published RSS metadata from Bloomberg Technology, Business, Industries and Markets feeds on a scheduled GitHub Actions workflow. Exact canonical article URLs are deduplicated. Articles are retained across successful collection runs; keyword-based inclusion and rule-based topic classification are provisional research labels, not assessments of editorial accuracy. Some stories may be missed between polling cycles, and a feed may be unavailable or rate-limited. The index therefore does not claim comprehensive Bloomberg AI coverage.

Chronology: retain the publisher's original RSS date, the archive's collection timestamp, and any later corrections as separate evidence. A feed-supplied date is a publication claim; independent archives and first-seen records can be used to assess disputes. Do not infer causation, misconduct or corporate coordination from similarities between articles.
