Packaging & label studio

Inspired by the supplied design board, the visual language combines ivory and cream paper, rose bouquets, sage-green satin-style ribbon, antique gold borders and formal British serif typography. Proposed components include rose-patterned cartons, embossed bars, botanical hang tags, secondary packaging and a box die-cut.

The images and SVG visual studies shown here are recreations based on the supplied references, not a claim that finished retail artwork, printing proofs or die-cut engineering files have been approved.

The supplied Etsy order screenshot confirms an order for a custom snap-bar silicone mould at £34.99. The brief requested curved corners where possible, a traditional elegant British serif (Baskerville or Caslon style), and three lines of branded text. The listing describes a food-safe choice.

Important distinction: the original botanical concept board features an oval soap with “Crystal Cleanse / Velvet Lather”, while the subsequently ordered custom mould specifies “Grace in Eden / Botanical Beauts / Rose Garden”. Both are retained as separate documented design stages rather than presented as identical.

The screenshot shows an estimated delivery date of 10 September. Order confirmation does not prove delivery or suitability for cosmetic manufacturing; delivery and the final physical mould require verification.

Packaging claims are not automatically verified

Statements shown in earlier mockups such as “natural ingredients”, “cruelty free”, “palm oil free” or “made in England” are draft design text. They should only appear on actual products when substantiated. The example ingredients panel on the concept art is also not an approved formulation or compliant final label.
