THE STORY BEHIND THE BRAND

Inspired by the beauty of a garden.

Founded as a product concept in 2026, Grace Eden is a garden-led soap and giftware identity. Botanical Beauts is the collection name, while Rose Garden, Apple Orchard, Herbal Meadow and Lavender Fields are proposed editions.

Our current stage includes actual mould-order specifications alongside design, material, regulatory and manufacturing research. The next steps are to confirm samples, formalise formulas and labels and test potential production relationships.

Prototype to approved product

Production development

  1. Confirm mould delivery and inspect dimensions, engraving and materials
  2. Finalise artwork, brand hierarchy and editable artwork files
  3. Work with an appropriate cosmetics formulator and safety specialist
  4. Test moulding, final appearance, labelling and packaging compatibility
  1. Prepare required UK cosmetic safety documentation and product information
  2. Confirm supplier and ingredient traceability, claims and batch records
  3. Calculate full cost per unit, minimum run and packaging quotations
  4. Trial small-scale demand and approved fulfilment before larger orders

Packaging claims are not automatically verified

Statements shown in earlier mockups such as “natural ingredients”, “cruelty free”, “palm oil free” or “made in England” are draft design text. They should only appear on actual products when substantiated. The example ingredients panel on the concept art is also not an approved formulation or compliant final label.
