Specialist and interdisciplinary funding

UKRI’s cross-council pilot committed £65 million across two rounds. Compare like-for-like specialist calls using award values, applications, success rates and inflation-adjusted budgets. To establish displacement, identify which budget lines funded the pilot and whether they were transferred from existing specialist calls.

Finding historical awards

The Dimensions Wizard for ORCID available-data list records funder coverage by country and time period. Use it to locate historical awards, then check the original funder’s record. Use the funder’s current call page for application status and eligibility.
