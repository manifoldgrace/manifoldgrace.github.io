How these suggestions work

The guide maps your chosen perspective and interests to a maintained list of existing public URLs. It does not score people, infer protected characteristics, make funding decisions, rank the worth of visitors, collect responses or modify the evidence. It makes no promise that an external programme is currently open or that a research initiative is completed.

Suggested future improvements include multilingual accessibility, independently tested assistive-technology support, a maintained broken-link audit, current-publication status labels and explicit evidence of outcomes. Any analytics or form submission would require separate privacy assessment and transparent consent.
