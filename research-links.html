STRUCTURED REFERENCE CORPUS

How to use this page

This is a navigation layer rather than a paper or essay: a numbered reference corpus for sources I return to across disciplines. The emphasis is on authoritative, primary or institutionally useful destinations that can anchor deeper reading rather than on producing a long undifferentiated bookmarks list.

The structure is intentionally expandable. New sources can be added inside the correct discipline without changing the page architecture, and the search box below filters across titles and descriptions while the numbered sections preserve the wider taxonomy.

RESEARCH NOTE

What this corpus represents

This page consolidates a research habit developed over many years, with source collection and cross-disciplinary learning reaching back to around 2008. It is not a claim that every site has been read exhaustively; it is a navigational map of the institutions, databases and reference environments that support how I verify, compare and extend what I know.

What I derive from the corpus is less a pile of facts than a method: compare primary and secondary sources, understand the institutional setting around a claim, trace provenance, distinguish evidence from interpretation, and connect technical questions to legal, economic, historical, theological and social systems rather than studying them in isolation.
