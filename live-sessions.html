OWNER UPLOAD

Upload a clip

This uploader is prepared for individual clips in common web and mobile video formats, including MP4, WebM, MOV / QuickTime, M4V and OGV. A browser-side check gives immediate feedback where video metadata can be read, while the backend performs the authoritative validation after upload.

The upload credential is entered only when needed and is kept in memory for the current page session; it is never written into this repository or saved to browser storage. The video itself is uploaded directly to the media provider over HTTPS, so the signing service does not have to relay a large video file.

EDITORIAL STANDARD

Context, provenance & disputes

Short clips can distort a longer discussion if they are detached from the question being answered or the sequence of the exchange. For that reason, this archive should record dates and context where known and should distinguish a direct quotation or visible event from an interpretation of what the exchange means.

If a claim made in a debate is disputed, the archive can retain the clip while separately noting the strength of the available evidence: documented, highly probable, probable, plausible, unresolved or contradicted. The purpose is preservation and analysis, not presenting every statement made in a live discussion as established fact.
