Working principles under consideration

  • Results and quality: Agree realistic milestones and evaluate completed work fairly, with collaboration encouraged.
  • Flexible working: Explore an 11am–4pm core collaboration window where appropriate, with overall hours, pay and availability defined for each position and respecting working-time obligations.
  • Family and wellbeing: Accommodate caring responsibilities, accessibility needs, remote or hybrid work where feasible, and reasonable time off.
  • Initiative: Welcome ideas, sound judgment, helpful AI use and constructive problem-solving, with information-security and confidentiality safeguards.
  • Feedback: Address issues with clear expectations, support and a fair procedure rather than automatic punitive 'three strikes'.
  • Long-term contribution: Value continuity, appropriate progression, sustainable remuneration and rewarding high-quality work.

How future recruitment would work

Applications would be assessed for competence, relevant experience, potential, professional conduct and the actual requirements of each role. There would be no age or ethnicity quotas for hiring. The mix of generations and backgrounds in the visual team concept communicates an inclusive aspiration, not selection preferences.

Any future recruitment process should provide a privacy notice, lawful retention schedule, clear candidate consent where appropriate, secure access controls and a straightforward process to request deletion or correction of candidate records. Applicants should not send CVs or sensitive documents through the general website enquiry form at this stage.
