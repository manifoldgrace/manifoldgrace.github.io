PERSONAL NOTE

What I am doing now is not pursuing more learning for learning's sake, more restless busyness or partying, more weightlifting, longer hours, more certificates, more attention to my hair, the accumulation of more money, houses or cars, or another collection of hobbies; it is interceding and seeking God's face, and allowing that pursuit to order what comes next.

I still have very much to do, but I cannot move in every direction at once. Understanding sequence and season is part of wisdom: recognising what belongs to this moment, what can wait, and what should be allowed to mature before the next thing is built.

The work of uncovering truths hidden within history continues for us all, but it cannot become a reason to stand still. Understanding the past should deepen discernment and sharpen direction without suspending forward progress.

At a time when the information age — and especially social media — can seem to speak from every direction at once, I believe it is even more important to draw nearer to God and hear what He has to say first. Before adding more noise, more movement or more ambition, I want discernment to begin there.

Success, to me, means achieving meaningful goals, building wealth and security, earning recognition where work warrants it, having a strong sense of identity and moving with clear direction. I count myself fortunate to have worked and learned in environments carrying prestige, responsibility and high standards across finance, media, public service, education, technology, sport and the arts. I regard that exposure as favour rather than entitlement, and where I describe myself as a leader I mean something observable: coaching, mentoring, facilitating, coordinating people and systems, advocating publicly, teaching, and repeatedly accepting responsibility for work that affects others.

My early career grew from music, live sound, media and audiovisual work into technology, operations, education, governance, AI ethics and research. I consider myself accomplished in the literal sense that I have completed and contributed to meaningful work across several environments, while also being very much in progress. Some projects have been paused by practical, administrative or life constraints rather than abandoned; others remain in the pipeline. My opinions will become more visible as I consolidate them carefully. I am deeply grateful to everyone who has helped me along the way. Love, Grace x
