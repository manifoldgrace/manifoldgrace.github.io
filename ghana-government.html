How the structure fits together

Ghana’s national institutions are set out separately from regional coordination and local assembly administration. Sector bodies below are grouped by subject to help route enquiries; this does not place independent regulators, constitutional institutions or public corporations under a minister’s operational control.

Source notes and coverage

This directory covers every ministry in the 23-ministry structure and all sixteen regions. The 261 assembly entries reproduce the names and routes in the retrieved Local Government Service regional lists; individual assembly upgrades, names and websites can change. The public-body selection provides operational routes across the sectors; the official directories above extend coverage to additional organisations and officers.

Office-holder and email sources are recorded separately. Regional names draw on the published appointment record, with council pages supplying contacts. Eastern, Greater Accra and Northern regional enquiries have a clearly labelled Local Government Service referral route where a current council mailbox was not established from the retrieved official pages. The Chief Justice’s office email is published in the linked Judicial Service brochure, dated 2024.

Ministry names reflect the government structure and current sector usage. Some official websites and directories retain earlier portfolio names. The August appointment notices take precedence over older personnel lists for the affected portfolios.

This is a public reference directory. It does not store private correspondence or send emails. The impact register separately records dated correspondence and any evidenced developments.
