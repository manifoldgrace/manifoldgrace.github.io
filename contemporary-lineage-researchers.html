Separate enquiries: collaboration and genealogy

Professional enquiry: introduce Manifold Grace, identify one linked publication and propose a focused exchange or possible collaboration. Genealogical enquiry: separately and respectfully ask whether the person is interested in checking historical family connections, without assuming kinship. Request no sensitive family records in a first message, and do not publish any response without permission.

Family relationship verification should proceed through consensually shared family names and places, archival records, parent–child links and other independently corroborated evidence. “Genealogical” concerns family descent; “geological” concerns earth science and rocks.
