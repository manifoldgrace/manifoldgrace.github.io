# Public editorial policy

Publish finished, approved material and functioning features. Do not promote unfinished work, advertise future releases, display development badges, or add coming-soon and provisional-cover copy.

Book covers and existing descriptions may be displayed as requested by Grace without process commentary or disabled promotional controls. Do not imply that an unpublished edition is published or available to buy. Only add purchase links and publication claims when supported by a verified available edition.

Keep unpublished drafts and incomplete features private. Empty release sections stay hidden until a verified published item exists. Preserve factual research uncertainty, external policy timetables, personal planning tools and accurate records of ongoing correspondence; these are not product promotion.
