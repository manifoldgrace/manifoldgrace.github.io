London Smart Piezoelectric Flooring Pilot Dossier

Turning London's Footsteps into Clean Energy proposes a smart-city pilot combining piezoelectric energy harvesting with AI-optimised infrastructure management in high-footfall London public spaces.

The published record covers technical specifications, accessibility, materials and drainage engineering, AI integration, ESG alignment and public-space deployment. The canonical DOI is 10.5281/zenodo.17439725.

PREPRINT · ZENODO · 2025

CESI v1.0 – The Chronological Ethical Self-Inventory

The Chronological Ethical Self-Inventory (CESI) is a reflective framework for mapping life experiences across education, work and society. It combines chronology, ethics and philosophy as a structured process of accountability and applied learning.

The Zenodo record presents CESI as an open preprint and conceptual framework. Its canonical DOI is 10.5281/zenodo.17266802.
