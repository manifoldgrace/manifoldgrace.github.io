London Smart Piezoelectric Flooring Pilot Dossier

Turning London's Footsteps into Clean Energy proposes a smart-city pilot combining piezoelectric energy harvesting with AI-optimised infrastructure management in high-footfall London public spaces.

The published record covers technical specifications, accessibility, materials and drainage engineering, AI integration, ESG alignment and public-space deployment. The Zenodo record remains the canonical version and carries DOI 10.5281/zenodo.17439725.
