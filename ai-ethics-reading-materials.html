Ethics of AI · Essay development

Distinctive ethical challenges posed by AI

An interdisciplinary enquiry drawing on utilitarianism, deontology and social constructivism, considering healthcare, governance, employment, privacy and autonomy. The drafts develop the relationship between ethical frameworks and cross-sector consequences.

Ethics of AI · Algorithmic bias

Bias in AI datasets

An essay draft examining how classification, data collection and model development can reproduce inequalities. It considers statistical and ethical bias, responsibility throughout the development lifecycle and the limits of purely technical fixes.
