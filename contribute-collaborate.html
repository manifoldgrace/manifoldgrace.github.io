Contribute & Collaborate

Different abilities. Different ways to participate. Shared benefit where possible, without obligation.

Our principle: Manifold Grace welcomes people and organisations who choose freely to contribute according to their abilities, interests, experience, availability and resources. Each collaboration should have an agreed purpose, clearly understood expectations, appropriate recognition and fair, mutually beneficial terms, while advancing knowledge or public benefit. No one is expected to help, work without agreement, or make commitments beyond what they choose.

Current position: This is an invitation to explore possibilities, not a request for unpaid labour or a claim that funded positions, partnerships or events already exist.

What voluntary and mutually beneficial means here

Voluntary choice: Expressing interest is not a commitment. People are free to decline, ask questions, set boundaries or decide that an opportunity does not suit them.

No automatic unpaid work: An introductory conversation or idea does not authorise Manifold Grace to use someone's labour or work. Volunteer activity must be genuinely voluntary, with a suitable defined scope; professional work may require a contract and pay. A promise of exposure is not a substitute for fair payment where payment is appropriate.

Ability-based and inclusive: Responsibilities should match capabilities, access needs, learning goals and availability. A participant need not fit a single professional category or commit to an ongoing schedule.

Mutual benefit is discussed, not assumed: Possible benefits include knowledge sharing, fair credit, approved promotion, educational experience, access to research, networks or payment when funded. Some contributions may primarily serve the public good; that must be understood and freely chosen.

Ownership and recognition: Before work begins, agree authorship, attribution, confidentiality, data protection, use of outputs, editorial review, recording consent and rights to publish or reuse material. Independently created work is not transferred by merely discussing a possible collaboration.

Realistic expectations: Opportunities may be exploratory, unfunded or awaiting governance and resources. There is currently no standing promise of placements, paid roles, public speaking commissions, research publication or access to a Zoom event.

Safe and accountable conduct: Respectful communication, reasonable adjustments and safeguarding are essential where relevant. Personal or sensitive information should not be submitted through initial enquiries.

How a collaboration may develop

  1. Explore: Identify a shared interest or problem worth investigating.
  2. Clarify: Discuss the person's abilities, interests, time, accessibility requirements and desired outcomes.
  3. Agree: Define responsibilities, boundaries, duration, expenses or fees, ownership and attribution before substantive work begins.
  4. Contribute: Work only within the agreed scope, with room for feedback and fair correction.
  5. Review: Evaluate the result, acknowledge contributions appropriately and decide independently whether to continue.

No offer, employment relationship, official affiliation or publication agreement is created by submitting an expression of interest.
