Art

A portfolio page for painting and visual work, with enough explanation to show how a piece was made without turning each work into a long essay.

PORTFOLIO METHOD

How work will be presented

Each finished work can be shown with a concise process record: medium, scale, subject or research prompt, key decisions, technique and what changed between the initial idea and the finished piece. That gives the viewer more than an image while keeping the page visually calm.

Where a work draws on history, theology, technology, landscape or another research area, the relevant source can be linked without implying that every creative choice needs an academic justification.

SELECTED WORK

Painting & visual experiments

No painting series is published on Manifold Grace yet. When work is added, the page will favour a small number of strong images and expandable process notes rather than a dense wall of thumbnails.

This leaves room for the visual language to evolve without locking the portfolio into an aesthetic that later work may outgrow.
