# Research Question and Gap Validation — Project Template
**Status:** Reusable research-design template; not evidence of an implemented experiment.

## Purpose
Create a falsification-oriented research protocol for one discipline, distinguishing the research problem, strongest established work, unresolved limitation, and contribution under test.

## Required sections
1. Discipline and precise research question
2. Prior art and strongest competing findings (DOI/URL, publication year)
3. Claim-to-evidence and contradiction matrix
4. Falsifiable hypothesis and null hypothesis where appropriate
5. Data, ethics, permissions and methods
6. Planned tests, comparison baseline and analysis
7. Reproducibility artefacts and limitations
8. Outcome status: proposed / in progress / validated / rejected

## Deliverables
A protocol, evidence index, evaluation plan and reproducible reporting template.

_Adapt for the selected discipline; no data, results or project completion are asserted by this file._
