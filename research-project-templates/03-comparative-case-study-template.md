# Comparative AI Case Study — Project Template
**Status:** Reusable case-study template; not a finished case study.

## Purpose
Compare a documented application of AI in a scientific or social discipline with its claimed impact, independent evidence and alternative approaches.

## Case record
- Case title, location, period, organisation and responsible stakeholders
- AI model, version and task
- Original primary-source URL and independent-source URLs
- Research context and pre-existing baseline
- Deployment constraints and meaningful human oversight
- Outcomes actually measured, compared with publicly claimed outcomes
- Ethics, privacy, safety, failure modes and potential bias
- Transferability to other settings, costs and uncertainties
- Lessons that are falsifiable in a follow-up study

## Deliverables
Evidence table, methods and limitations, comparison matrix, case-study report.

_A blank starting point; no case-study work or empirical finding is claimed._
