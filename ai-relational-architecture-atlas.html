02 / Common relational reasoning frameworks

A shared framework should provide interoperability where meaningful, not impose one representation or model on every task. It must not be conflated with the solution being evaluated. Candidates are listed by their likely scope and evidence limitations.

Native observationsArchitecture-specific extractionTyped relation contractContext and uncertainty analysisIndependent QA

FR-01 · Typed relation and context graph

Across most families: subject, relation, object, polarity, domain, context and conditions. A graph records claims but does not prove them.

FR-02 · Temporal event ledger

Especially forecasting, robotics and automation: event order, valid times, versions, state changes and corrections. Sequence does not imply causality.

FR-03 · Statistical and causal-hypothesis layer

Especially predictive analytics and intervention research: variables, populations, confounders, uncertainty and identifying assumptions. Causal claims require stronger evidence.

FR-04 · Constraints, permissions and obligations

Especially intelligent agents and workflow systems: permission checks, exceptions, actor roles, rule precedence and jurisdictional applicability.

FR-05 · Provenance, contradictions and revision

Across families: evidence identifiers, derivations, competing findings, responsible actors, model and dataset versions, recorded corrections.

Adoption criterion: A common framework must preserve domain-specific meaning and demonstrate useful improvements against independently specified tests. Over-generalisation is an evaluated failure, not a benefit.

04 / Further columns for a research-grade register

The first table prioritises legibility. A second-stage expansion should add: input modality; training objective; supervision mode; parameterisation; inference mechanism; memory/state representation; output modality; deployment context; edge/cloud constraints; data provenance; intended use; limitations; model/dataset versions; evaluation dataset; result metrics; uncertainty; safety controls; legal applicability; system owner; evidence links; reviewer and reassessment trigger. Do not fill fields with guesses or treat missing research as completed.

Keep numerical performance and compliance claims separate from architecture categories. A research candidate without a prototype must have an explicit not tested status.
