Ga community development

I am building connections within the UK Ga community and across Ghana and the wider diaspora by sharing cultural footage, useful links and media contacts, exchanging knowledge, and identifying opportunities for heritage research and language learning.

This page records my contribution as the work develops: making resources easier to find, connecting generations and supporting the preservation and understanding of Ga-Dangme heritage.

Community-building activity log

20 OCTOBER 2026 · RESOURCE SHARING

Connecting UK family and community with Ghanaian cultural media

I gathered and organised TikTok handles, links, screenshots and videos covering Ga-Dangme culture, traditional ceremonies, local news and Ghanaian public updates. I prepared a compact WhatsApp directory and shared resources within my family/community network.

Contribution: resource discovery, classification and sharing to support cultural connection between the UK, Ghana and the diaspora.

Evidence: supplied resource batches, the prepared directory, Grace's activity account and a private reply acknowledging the resources. The original messages and contact details are not reproduced publicly.

20 OCTOBER 2026 · RECIPROCAL KNOWLEDGE EXCHANGE

New learning and networking routes suggested

A family/community contact responded positively, offered to check the resources, and suggested women's professional meetups, Ga-language lessons, public-affairs channels and a potential future project or event meeting.

Outcome so far: one evidenced two-way exchange and five follow-up leads. Group membership, attendance, introductions and partnerships have not yet been recorded.
