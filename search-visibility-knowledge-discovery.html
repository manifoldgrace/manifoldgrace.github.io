AI governance, digital rights and evidence-based research guides

Manifold Grace is an independent interdisciplinary research and creative-practice website. This guide helps readers locate existing projects, papers, case studies and learning materials through questions they may use when researching AI ethics, UK institutions, digital evidence and societal outcomes. It is an editorial navigation page, not a claim that these topics are currently the most searched.

How these topics are selected and maintained

Topic phrases reflect the questions and vocabulary relevant to existing Manifold Grace work. They are not verified Google search-volume rankings. Future prioritisation should compare actual queries and impressions in Google Search Console with UK interest over time in Google Trends, then refine content where an original, useful answer can be provided.

Site discovery and editorial standards

Each substantive page should have a unique, accurate title and short description; meaningful headings; relevant internal links with descriptive anchor text; accessible image descriptions; usable mobile layout; and sources appropriate to its claims. Search phrasing is included only where the corresponding content exists. Original research, research proposals and externally validated outcomes must remain clearly distinguished.
