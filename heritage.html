Heritage

My heritage is being reconstructed through two principal family corridors: a paternal Ga-Dangme line rooted in Accra, Ghana, and a maternal English line that includes both biological ancestry and legally established adoptive-family history.

DOCUMENTED / UNDER RESEARCH

01 — Ghanaian corridor

My paternal family is rooted in coastal Accra, with family associations across Jamestown, Gbese, Ngleshie-Alata, Swalaba and Sonmenaa. Recent generations and family geography are supported through family records and oral history; the exact bridge into earlier generations remains under reconstruction.

My grandfather carried out substantial genealogical work on elders, descendants, family branches and the wider Asafoatse Nettey family tradition. That body of research is an important starting point for the present archive.

DOCUMENTED / SUPPORTED

02 — English corridor

My maternal heritage includes English biological ancestry alongside a significant legal-adoptive corridor through the Symondson family. The adoptive relationship is kept distinct from unresolved biological-parentage research.

The wider Symondson record includes musical, military and civic histories. A notable collateral relative, Henry Symondson (1832–1911), is recorded as a musician and Staff Sergeant in the Royal South Middlesex Militia.

SUPPORTED COLLATERAL CONNECTION

03 — Deakin, Beaufort & Plantagenet connection

A collateral branch of the adoptive Symondson family married into the Deakin family. Published genealogy for that branch traces through the Beaufort family to John of Gaunt and Edward III.

This is presented as a collateral adoptive-family connection, not as evidence that my grandmother was a biological descendant of the Beaufort or Plantagenet lines.

UNDER RESEARCH

04 — Wider historical connections

The English corridor also intersects with histories of law, public service, military service, music and civic life. Proposed connections involving families such as Dolben, Digby and Noel remain under investigation and are not treated as established lineage here.
