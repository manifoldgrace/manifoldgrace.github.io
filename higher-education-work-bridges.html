Research directory · verified 11 November 2026. Organisations here operate at different points between academic study, scientific research, employer needs, workplace competence, professional recognition and public value. No one body has sole authority to define intellectual excellence, workplace performance or all forms of societal value.

How to interpret the table: Academic standards and professional competence are related but not interchangeable. Employer-defined measures of value do not automatically substitute for scholarly validity, and university qualifications do not automatically grant workplace authority. A contact address is evidence of a published enquiry route, not endorsement of this research or an offer to evaluate an individual's qualifications.

Where a bridge does not remove the institutional boundary

University: disciplinary mastery, research methods, academic independence, conceptual precision and scholarship. Professional bodies: profession-specific competencies, ethics and accreditation. Employer: organisational operation, performance, applied judgment and approved decision-making authority. Public bodies and sector intermediaries: labour market evidence, policy coordination, learning standards, funding and employer–university collaboration.

Questions for a comparative research audit

  1. Which organisations set qualifications, provide optional guidance, fund research, accredit professional practice or merely convene partners?
  2. How are knowledge, applied competence, performance and social value separately defined and measured?
  3. Where can prior work experience, independent research and technical portfolios substitute for formal entry routes, if permitted?
  4. Which disciplines require unique methods, and which general reasoning skills can transfer without distortion?
  5. Can an external industry framework influence university assessment while preserving disciplinary and academic independence?
  6. How can students and professionals challenge inaccurate competency assessments and document demonstrable contributions?
