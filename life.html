Privacy by design

This page is intentionally retrospective and selective. It should not reveal a home address, routine precise enough to predict where I will be, or travel information that functions as real-time location sharing.

That gives the site a sense of personality and continuity while preserving the anonymity I still want around the parts of life that do not need to become public evidence.
