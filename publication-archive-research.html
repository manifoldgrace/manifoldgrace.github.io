Research paper initiatives

WORKING PAPER A · DESIGN SCIENCE / SOFTWARE ARCHITECTURE

Provenance-preserving period-based public publication archives

Specifies the extraction pipeline, dated record model, daily-to-annual PDF generation, machine-readable exports, public interfaces, architecture evaluation and falsifiable completeness tests. Explicitly distinguishes dated work logs from original dates of externally linked publications.

WORKING PAPER B · COMPARATIVE LEGAL AND PUBLIC POLICY ANALYSIS

Public-interest publication accessibility and archival duties

Investigates the feasibility, proportionality and safeguards of permanent publication directories, regular accessible bundles, correction records and historical transparency. The suggested duty is a research proposal, not a universal obligation currently established in law.

WORKING PAPER C · EDUCATION / AI EVALUATION / KNOWLEDGE CONTINUITY

Living evidence archives for higher education and advanced AI evaluation

Examines how original dated sources, accessible archives, correction histories and code-based exercises might complement textbooks, foster evidence literacy and strengthen longitudinal AI evaluation. Educational impact and long-term model reliability are hypotheses to test.

Project levels and measured outcomes

Beginner — understand and reproduce a small dated index

Read the architecture paper, inspect owned/public source log records, generate one day and one month, compare JSON against PDF links and identify the difference between a log date and a publisher's original release date. Evidence: annotated script, simple test and a source critique.

Intermediate — extend the archive and accessibility tests

Adapt an expressly permitted CMS export, validate dates and canonical URLs, detect duplicate entries, add corrections and publication statuses, audit keyboard navigation, compare HTML, JSON and PDF retrieval tasks, and document CI coverage. Evidence: modular implementation, controlled tests and reproducible benchmark.

Advanced — independent assurance and multi-source longitudinal evaluation

Use licensed or otherwise authorised publication catalogues with independent coverage audits; assess version changes, evidence contradictions, human oversight, preservation of withdrawn records without unlawful republication, cross-institution source metadata and historical educational/AI evaluation. Evidence: threat model, human-subjects ethics, statistical analysis, negative results and independent replication.

Limits, safeguards and reproducibility

This reference archive indexes Manifold Grace's public dated log, not all of its pages and not every article ever written. It does not train an AI model or validate model improvement. The generated PDFs are not independently certified to PDF/UA; alternative HTML and JSON/CSV are available for accessible and machine-oriented uses. Copyright, database rights, privacy and lawful access remain separate constraints.

Research checks should compare log entries to generated metadata, detect missing dated blocks, verify original-source links, inspect rendered PDF pages, perform independent accessibility assessment, review updated scripts and distinguish GitHub commit verification from live website publication.

Dated evidence and standards

Original source dates are retained where established: UK Intellectual Property Office copyright guidance, published 12 June 2014; WCAG 2.2 Recommendation, 12 December 2024; NISO JATS 1.4, published 31 October 2024. Crossref's living API documentation is presented without inventing a first publication date.
