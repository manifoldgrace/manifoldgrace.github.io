About

Manifold Grace is the public knowledge and portfolio space of Grace Rosemary Nettey — bringing together research, technology, education, music, public life, faith, heritage and creative practice within one connected identity.

CURRENT

Identity & overview

My work has developed across overlapping fields rather than through one professional lane. I am interested in how technology, institutions, culture, knowledge and human experience interact, and in the systems through which ideas become decisions, services and public outcomes.

Alongside professional practice, I maintain independent research and continuing professional development. Manifold Grace exists to make that work easier to understand, trace and explore.

Across disciplines

My experience spans technology and audiovisual systems, NHS and public-service environments, finance, media, sport, education, business operations, research and creative practice. Earlier work and study also include music technology, audio engineering, business and entrepreneurship, legal foundations, teaching and care-related learning.

Formal study has been non-linear, with periods of interruption, but research, skills development and professional learning have continued independently. I treat those experiences as connected dimensions of one body of work: technical, analytical, creative, educational and civic.

Public profiles

These profiles provide different parts of the public record: research identity, professional history, technical projects, visual research and a broader link hub. They are kept separate because no single platform represents the whole of the work.

Where possible, Manifold Grace acts as the central reference point and the external profiles provide corroborating context, specialist records or platform-specific material.
