Register your interest

Complete this short form to submit a project, request an assessment, volunteer as a reviewer or explore a collaboration. Registration is voluntary and does not imply endorsement, certification, approval or publication.

Please do not include patient records, personal case files, confidential evidence, passwords or other sensitive information. Submissions are handled through Formspree and sent to a private receiving inbox. No public profile is created automatically. You can request removal of your submitted details via the contact page.

Independent assessment

Manifold Grace is developing research approaches for examining claim-to-evidence coverage, provenance, historical records, data integrity and contradictions. The registration form above is an expression-of-interest route, not an accredited certification service or guarantee of review.

Any future assessment should define its evidence requirements, reviewer independence, privacy safeguards, limitations and a route to correct or challenge findings. AI may assist analysis; conclusions require appropriate human oversight.
