Project and paper initiative

Working research-paper designation: Coverage-Aware Visual Knowledge Auditing and Cross-Domain Discovery for Public-Interest AI.

Proposed question: Does a provenance-constrained, coverage-aware retrieval pipeline improve the reliability and representativeness of technical visual-knowledge audits and social-good project discovery relative to popular-result retrieval and generic vision-language summarisation?

Paper framework — reserved, not represented as completed

Research question and strongest prior art; predeclared corpus and access rights; datasets and sampling procedure; comparison baselines; independent ground-truth annotations; accuracy, confidence and coverage metrics; security and governance risks; ablations; negative results; reproducible code and archive provenance. This is a research designation, not a submitted manuscript or preprint.

Originality status: undetermined until the strongest comparable methods are evaluated. The distinguishing hypothesis concerns jointly assessing visual claims, omission-aware coverage, under-recognised participants, reproducible provenance and affordable system alternatives—not any individual component in isolation.
