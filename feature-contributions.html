Feature Requests & Contribution Register

A cross-platform historical register of my proposed product improvements, conceptual designs and external submissions, including responses and limitations. Original conceptual framing is distinguished from AI-assisted editing and from any recipient's later development.

Register expanded 3 November 2026 using accessible email correspondence, prior conversation records and existing site material. This is an evolving index, not a claim that every historic communication in every account has been exhaustively recovered.

How the record is assessed

Response categories: constructive technical explanation; favourable acknowledgement / referral; procedural receipt; substantive criticism or refusal; moderation / delivery obstacle; no verified reply. A courteous response is not proof of engineering acceptance, implementation, ownership transfer or causal influence. The same architecture sent to 30 recipients is one conceptual family with many outreach events, not 30 separately invented ideas.

Evidence: public proposal links are provided where known; private email threads are preserved in the author's mailbox rather than exposed on this public website. Dates identify documented submission or site-record dates, not necessarily first conception.
