PUBLIC-SOURCE RESEARCH SCAN · EVIDENCE CHECKED 5 NOVEMBER 2026

AI feature gaps: a 20-company research scan

This scan develops my research into where AI tools can better support evidence quality, user autonomy, correction, privacy and accountability. It compares existing capabilities with my earlier proposal families to identify useful contributions, rather than submit the same broad request repeatedly. A documented limitation can become an exploratory project, a reproducible test or a bottleneck analysis before it becomes a feature request.

Coverage: Twenty organisations selected from the existing outreach programme, including its AWS, IBM and NVIDIA records; this is not a market ranking. Five entries identify documented product boundaries, one records a checked public issue, and fourteen identify existing functionality with narrower extensions to investigate. These are evidence categories, not twenty proven missing features.

Method, scope and how to use the findings

Compared the existing evidence-verification proposal family and context-transparency requests with official documentation, feedback routes and selected public issue records. Public-source discovery was followed by direct checks of selected consequential limits and issue states. Sources below are the evidence trail for each entry.

This was a desk scan, not an exhaustive crawl, paid-account audit or live API experiment. Private support tickets, internal roadmaps and Discord histories were not inspected. An undocumented capability is not necessarily absent; a documented boundary can have a workaround. Issue states and product contracts are dated observations and may change.

Contribution sequence: identify the exact product and requirement; check existing issues and workarounds; reproduce with fictional or authorised data; separate observed behaviour from its explanation; write a bounded analysis; then refine the existing request or contribute to its current thread. Record submission, receipt, review and implementation separately.

No feature requests or messages were sent during this scan. Existing submission and moderation histories remain in their original entries; channel verification does not establish posting access, staff review or adoption.
