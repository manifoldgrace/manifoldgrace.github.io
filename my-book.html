Full Biography

0250 by 30
EXISTING CONTENT · BOOK IN DEVELOPMENT

50 by 30

A reflective book built from an existing record of fifty things pursued, achieved, commenced or deliberately experienced by thirty, with emphasis on what was meaningfully within my control rather than on comparison with somebody else's timetable.

The project combines personal milestones with the wider questions of timing, opportunity, interruption, agency, sequence and what it means to recognise progress without pretending that every desired outcome is equally controllable.

03Memoir
CONTENT EXISTS · IN DEVELOPMENT

Childhood & Young-Adult Memoir

A more personal narrative than the full biography, centred on childhood, young adulthood, family contribution, care, money, labour, education, identity, interruption and emotional experience.

The proposed method is to separate proved facts, estimates and interpretation so that memory is not made to carry evidential weight it cannot support on its own.

04Heritage
RESEARCH & MANUSCRIPT MATERIAL · IN DEVELOPMENT

Family History & Genealogical Record

A sourced family-history book bringing together the Ghanaian Ga-Dangme corridor and the English biological and adoptive-family corridors, with distinctions between documentary evidence, oral history, legal adoption, collateral connection and unresolved lineage.

The aim would be preservation as much as narrative: to retain names, places, documents, family research, provenance and evidential status so later generations can see both what is known and what still requires proof.

05Black History
SOURCE MATERIAL & DRAFT CONTENT · IN DEVELOPMENT

Black History Through Family & Public Records

A research-led work using existing family-history research, archival material and public records as a route into wider Black British and Ghanaian history rather than treating family history as detached from social history.

The concept would connect individual lives with migration, institutions, work, civic participation, land, education, culture and the changing ways Black histories are preserved or omitted.

HOW THIS PAGE SHOULD EVOLVE

From development shelf to publication shelf

The structure is designed to work much like a serious author site: each book can eventually have a full visual block, definitive cover, synopsis, extract, audiobook or talk clip, edition details and links to publishers, libraries, repositories or booksellers. The Manifold Grace version keeps the site's existing restrained black editorial language rather than adopting another author's branding.

Accuracy still matters more than polish: existing content is acknowledged as existing content, unpublished work is labelled unpublished, provisional titles remain provisional, and no “buy” or “order” controls appear until there is an edition genuinely available to obtain.
