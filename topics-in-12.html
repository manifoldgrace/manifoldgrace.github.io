Topics in 12

Twelve major social-science domains arranged as syllabus maps. Each domain remains distinct, while the card structure makes it easy to compare recurring ideas, methods and institutions across fields.

HOW TO READ THIS

Each square card is one syllabus component. Swipe or scroll horizontally as though turning through a compact set of study pages. Domains may exceed twelve cards where the syllabus requires it; numbering continues rather than forcing the field into an artificial limit.

SYNTHESIS LAYER

Cross-domain synthesis

The same object can be reopened through multiple domains. A university, for example, can be studied sociologically as an institution, politically as a governance structure, economically as a labour and funding system, psychologically as a learning environment, legally through rights and regulation, and methodologically through the quality of the evidence used to evaluate it.

The numbered cards make those overlaps visible without erasing the boundaries between disciplines.
