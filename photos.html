PROFESSIONAL PHOTO SET

Portfolio standard

The page is organised around useful image types rather than chronological posting. Each category holds no more than three images at this stage, keeping the presentation selective enough for professional viewing while allowing different sides of the work to be represented.

Images should be current enough to be recognisable, appropriately lit and free from unnecessary location or personal metadata. The goal is not to impose a physical ideal; it is to give different industries a clear, accurate visual reference.
