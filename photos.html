A restrained photo portfolio influenced by professional agency presentation but adapted for a multidisciplinary public profile. PROFESSIONAL PHOTO SET

Portfolio standard

The page is organised around useful image types rather than chronological posting. Categories run down the page, with small previews arranged side by side in each horizontal carousel. Select any image to view the full photograph. Earlier photographs remain available alongside newer additions, with edited studies identified in their captions.

Images should be current enough to be recognisable, appropriately lit and free from unnecessary location or personal metadata. The goal is not to impose a physical ideal; it is to give different industries a clear, accurate visual reference.
