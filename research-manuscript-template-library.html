Adapting to a journal or conference

These files are content structures, not counterfeit publisher templates. Before submitting, transfer the completed, suitable manuscript into the official LaTeX or Word author template of the selected venue; verify its current page limits, citation format, double-blind requirements, research ethics, accessibility, reproducibility checklist and disclosure rules.

See the high-tier publisher layout comparison and submission quality gates.

Publication and research integrity

Downloading a template does not create a research paper, academic qualification, publication or preprint. Add GitHub repository links only when a project exists, and Zenodo DOIs only when an eligible record has actually been deposited. Planned results must not be reported as findings.
