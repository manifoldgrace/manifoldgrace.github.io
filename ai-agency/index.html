Before a workflow goes live
Access, quality & accountability
Agree which data and tools an agent can access, what it can change, how success is measured and where exceptions go. Test output quality, recovery and running costs before expanding the scope.
Approvals & specialist decisions
Payments, contracts, public claims, production changes and regulated decisions require appropriate authorisation. Agents can prepare supporting work; clinical, legal or financial professional judgement and formal sign-off remain with authorised people.
Pricing & availability
Pricing and delivery depend on complexity, integrations, usage and support needs. An expression of interest starts a feasibility discussion; it does not activate agents or commit you to a purchase.

Request another workflow or a combination of services. We can assess what could be automated, what needs your approval and what evidence a pilot should produce.
