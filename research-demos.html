Limitations, controls and next engineering gates

All functions use deterministic browser JavaScript; no third-party credentials, background tracking or upload are required. The calculations have no statistical calibration. Before remote source ingestion, add a server-side permission boundary, data retention rules, prompt-injection tests, source validation, human approval, and evaluated model behaviour.
