How a collaboration could work

1. Identify the question. Define the problem, intended users and expected value.

2. Establish feasibility. Review technical constraints, available evidence, responsible-AI considerations and ownership of intellectual property.

3. Agree a bounded pilot. Set scope, responsibilities, documentation, consent, funding and success measures before committing work.

4. Test and assess. Evaluate usability, safety, quality, accessibility and relevant outcomes.

5. Decide the next stage. Document findings and determine whether to improve, scale, transfer or close the pilot.
