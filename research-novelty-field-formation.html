What does “new research field” actually mean?

Publication in 2026 establishes a document's date, not that the subject never existed earlier. Originality must be tested against predecessors, parallel discovery, terminology changes and the social history of research. This register identifies documented 2026 formulations; it does not claim to prove that entire disciplines were absent from all human knowledge before 2026.

TypeEvidence required
Newly named specialismDocumented formulation and earlier uses of the term
New research questionPrior-art search and a defined difference from earlier problems
New method or architectureComparison with prior methods, reproducible results and limitations
New interdisciplinary programmeSustained shared problems, community, terminology and methods
New scientific findingEvidence, critical review and independent replication where suitable

Named research paradigm formulated in 2026 · 21 July 2026 (online)

Agentic genomics

Original authors: Manuel Corpas, Heinner Guio and Segun Fatumo · Cell Genomics

What changed: A clearly articulated 'agentic genomics' paradigm: autonomous agents coordinate multi-step genomic analyses and shift validation into a central research challenge.

Prior work, unresolved questions and novelty assessment

Earlier foundations: Genomic workflows, bioinformatics, automation and AI-assisted analysis existed previously.

Still unresolved: Reproducibility, equitable validation, deployment boundaries and clinical-grade assurance.

Classification: Credible newly articulated subfield; not a wholly unprecedented subject.

How to investigate a claimed new field

  1. Define: State the name, scope, ontology, methods and claimed novelty.
  2. Trace: Search historical academic publications, patents, proceedings, archives, multilingual sources and earlier terminology.
  3. Credit: Record originators, contributors, parallel work and earlier partial solutions.
  4. Compare: Separate novelty of a label, problem, implementation, method and finding.
  5. Test: Examine evidence, reproducibility, limitations, expert disagreement and alternative explanations.
  6. Observe formation: Monitor conferences, programmes, standards, interdisciplinary collaborations and adoption.
  7. Archive: Preserve source dates, correction history, status changes and unresolved questions for successor researchers.
Knowledge-graph and information-architecture relationships

Represent each research formulation as an entity with its authors, publication date, source, predecessor concepts, related fields, methods, findings and evaluation status. Relations might include derived from, renames, extends, disputes, replicates, institutionalises and supersedes. Dates should distinguish first proposal, preprint, peer-reviewed publication and institutional adoption.

Relevance to global AI governance

AI identifiability, governance of autonomous agents and research provenance are promising areas of new combinations. Their underlying methods—asset registers, identity systems, verification, ontology engineering and risk management—precede 2026. A defensible new contribution must explain what existing approaches fail to do and what a new method does differently.
